View Itineraries Stay In Modern, Sustainably Designed Lodges Along the route, Explora’s mountain lodges offer carefully placed pauses within the Altiplano. In Chile, the journey begins at Explora Atacama before continuing into Bolivia, where two intimate mountain lodges in the Ramaditas and Chituca communities, together with Explora Jirira on the edge of the Uyuni Salt Flats, were designed by an award-winning Chilean architect. Modest in scale and intentionally remote, each lodge responds to its surroundings, supporting the rhythm of the travesía while keeping the landscape in focus. View Lodges Discover Immersive Explorations Traversing some of the world’s most extreme and extraordinary landscapes is enriched by expertly guided Explorations. With Explora’s multilingual guides and local drivers, each day adapts to your interests and fitness levels, unfolding across the Altiplano’s three distinct altitude zones. High deserts give way to volcanic slopes, vast salt flats, quiet lagoons, and remote valleys, experienced through walks, open-road drives, bike rides, and unhurried moments in between.
