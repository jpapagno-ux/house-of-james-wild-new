Watch Video Play Video Strategically placed on the bend of the Sand River in the most exclusive quadrant of South Africa’s Sabi Sand region with sweeping water-rich views from every area, there are nine enormous, stilted hideaways, combining design with traditional safari elements that blend seamlessly with the landscape. Here there is an evident awareness of an accord between modernity and primal African aesthetics. The private rim-flow pool and deck provide the perfect platform from which to start your game viewing experience. The wrap-around glass throughout provides clear views of the surrounding bush and once inside, there is a sense of seclusion which adds to the secretive, quiet charm that enhances your ability to commune with the natural world around you. If you’re a keen photographer, it’s a good idea to keep your camera handy as it’s not uncommon for hippos, giraffes or feisty warthogs to make surprise appearances. As the saying goes, you can always “Expect the unexpected’. Food & Dining Dining is a central part of the experience, combining refined cuisine with the atmosphere of the African bush. Menus change daily and blend modern culinary techniques with local South African flavours, using fresh and seasonal ingredients. Meals are thoughtfully woven into the rhythm of the safari day. Served in a variety of settings from the lodge’s river-facing dining areas and outdoor decks to traditional firelit bomas, or privately on the deck of your suite, creating dining experiences that are both relaxed and quietly memorable. A well-stocked bar and mixologists provide craft cocktails, local gins, curated premium wines, while the kitchen caters to a wide range of dietary preferences and requirements. Wellness & Activities Keeping fit and relaxed is easy with a visit to the pristine well-being area, where you can indulge in a workout or swim a few lengths in the centrally-located outdoor pool. We encourage guest participation in the variety of activities on offer so you can make the most of your time in a truly immersive way. You are free to choose from ever-exhilarating sunrise or sunset drives across 10 500ha of pristine wilderness, or alternatively the interpretive bush and nature walks and star gazing. Birdwatching is another popular choice with over 500 species to be found. Chat to your lodge manager or guide to get a better idea of what’s on offer and suitable for the current weather. It is a quiet luxury to find that your only decisions are selecting handcrafted treasures from the Safari Shop or choosing the perfect moment for an aromatherapy massage. 1 / 10 Next Prev Info Useful Information Health Guests travelling to the Greater Kruger region are encouraged to consult their physician or travel clinic prior to departure to discuss recommended vaccinations and malaria prevention. While the area is considered low risk, simple precautions such as using insect repellent and wearing long sleeves in the evening are advised. Guests should also travel with any required personal medications and ensure appropriate travel insurance is in place. The lodge team is always available to assist should any medical needs arise during your stay. Weather The Greater Kruger enjoys a warm, inviting climate year-round, making it a perfect safari destination in every season. Summer (October - April) brings warm days, often in the high 20s to mid-30s °C, with dramatic afternoon thunderstorms that refresh the bush and awaken vibrant birdlife and newborn wildlife. Winter (May - September) offers crisp mornings and mild, sunlit days, with minimal rainfall and open vistas that make game viewing on the river and nearby waterholes especially rewarding. Getting there By Air Guests can fly from Johannesburg or Cape Town into Skukuza Airport, situated within the Kruger National Park, followed by a short 15 - 20 minute road transfer to the lodge. Alternatively, Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport offers convenient access to the reserve. Private charter flights to nearby airstrips are also available. By Road The lodge is accessible via a scenic five hour drive from Johannesburg along established routes into the reserve, offering a gradual transition from city to wilderness. For further details and personalised arrangements, kindly contact the concierge team. Children Children under 12 are not permitted unless the lodge is booked exclusively. For young guests, supervised activities are available, but safety remains a priority at all times. Children aged 12 to 16 may join standard game drives and nature walks, while specialist walking safaris are reserved for adults. Guests booking a private vehicle may bring children of any age on game drives, with access to sightings guided by the discretion of our guides. Childminders, booked on the day of arrival, can be arranged allowing parents to enjoy uninterrupted moments in the bush.
