See select accolades below. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS How far is Anantara Ubud Bali Resort from Ubud centre? Anantara Ubud Bali Resort is 20 km away, or approximately 30 minutes by car. The resort provides a complimentary shuttle service to Ubud Centre subject to daily schedule. How far is Anantara Ubud Bali Resort from the airport? Anantara Ubud Bali Resort is an approximately 3-hours drive from Ngurah Rai International Airport, with limousine transfers available upon request. What is there to see near Anantara Ubud Bali Resort? Anantara Ubud Bali Resort is surrounded by some of the island’s most iconic destinations, from rice terraces and temples to waterfalls and mountains. How far is Anantara Ubud Bali Resort from Kintamani? Anantara Ubud Bali Resort is a 30-minute drive from Kintamani. Is Anantara Ubud Bali Resort’s accommodation accessible? Anantara Ubud Bali Resort is pleased to offer wheelchair-accessible accommodations and facilities. What are the dining options at Anantara Ubud Bali Resort? There are two restaurants at Anantara Ubud Bali Resort, Kirana and Amerta as well as two pool bars and a Lobby Lounge. What recreational facilities are there at Anantara Ubud Bali Resort? Anantara Ubud Bali Resort has two heating infinity swimming pools, a gym, yoga deck, the award-winning Anantara Spa, and kids’ and teens’ clubs. Does Anantara Ubud Bali Resort operate as normal on Nyepi? Yes, Anantara Ubud Bali Resort is fully operational on Nyepi. Nyepi In Bali – About Bali’s Day of Silence Nyepi, the Balinese "Day of Silence," will take place from 6:00 am on 19 March 2026 to 6:00 am on 20 March 2026. During this peaceful time, guests are invited to fully relax within the resort. Outdoor activities, including check-ins and check-outs, will be suspended. For the tranquility of the day, please keep music and TV volumes low. The island will observe a complete pause in traffic and transportation, and Ngurah Rai International Airport will be closed. Emergency services will continue to operate, ensuring a calm and harmonious experience across the island.
