Read More Mara Toto Tree Camp offers an incredibly private and tranquil safari. It accommodates a maximum of 8 guests in four luxurious treetop suites. Constructed from canvas and wood, set on elevated decks into the tree canopy, and boasting spacious living areas, it exudes an aura of exploration in its style. Each suite offers guests a luxurious bath, indoor shower, writing desk, and lounge, creating a sense that one could easily settle in and stay for days. While with us, why not tour our state-of-the-art kitchen, enjoy a relaxing spa treatment or browse the Great Plains Safari Boutique? Safari Suite Mara Toto Tree Camp offers four light and airy, spacious canvas tree canopy tented suites, each with a private lounge, light weights, yoga mat, stationary exercise bicycle and an outdoor verandah. The en-suite bathroom is complete with double vanity basins, a deep copper bath, and a large indoor shower. As each suite is on a raised deck built up into the canopy of the trees, and offers an astonishing view of the surrounding bush and wildlife passing by. You can capture your Maasai Mara safari memories using a complimentary professional camera set and lenses provided for you to use throughout your stay. At the end of your safari stay, our team will download your images and videos for you to take home at no additional cost. Begin Your Journey Lasting Impact Big Cats Initiative: Protecting Habitat for Big Cats across Africa Big Cats need land now more than ever. There are an estimated 44 million acres of land in Africa on which big cats roam. This land is unprotected or under hunting management. Great Plains Conservation and our Foundation’s response to this crisis is to support and secure habitats outside national parks. We work in partnership with local communities and other conservation stakeholders. Great Plains founders Dereck and Beverly Joubert established the Big Cats Initiative, funding multiple projects to preserve land for big cats and reduce human-wildlife conflict. Over a decade later, the Big Cats Initiative has funded 150 projects in 27 countries. We look for good ideas that we can help scale up to prevent and reverse the decline of big cats.
