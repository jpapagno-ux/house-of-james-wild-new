 A Journey through our Cabins 
 Indulge in Indonesian Elegance
 Authentic colors, textures, and styles derived from the cultures they embody endow each of the five cabin with a relaxed yet distinctive atmosphere. Spacious bathing facilities are tastefully integrated into the decor providing in a gracious well considered manner those amenities so essential to a comfortable standard of living. 
 Our cabin are more than just places to rest your head; they are your personal sanctuaries amid the vast Indonesian expanse. Each cabin reflects the mesmerizing diversity of this country, offering a glimpse into the rich tapestry of Indonesian culture.

 ASMAT CABIN
 The Asmat cabin is a spacious dwelling measuring 4 meters by 6.6 meters. Adorned with original art from the Asmat culture, this cabin provides ample room for your comfort. Here, you'll find a king-size bed designed for two adult passengers, ensuring a comfortable and luxurious experience. With meticulous attention to detail, this cabin offers a unique atmosphere that captures the essence of the Asmat culture.
 JAVA CABIN
 The Java cabin boasts original art from Java's rich culture. This spacious retreat measures 4 meters by 6.6 meters, providing ample space for your relaxation. It features a king-size bed, providing a haven for two adult passengers. Immerse yourself in the allure of Indonesian culture and enjoy the distinctive ambiance of this meticulously appointed cabin.
 BALI CABIN
 Step into the Bali cabin, named after the famed "Island of the Gods," and you'll be surrounded by original Balinese art. This spacious dwelling measures 4 meters by 5.6 meters, offering a generous space to unwind. It features a king-size bed for two adult passengers, providing comfort and luxury in a setting that celebrates the rich heritage of Bali. Each detail in this cabin reflects the island's unique charm and elegance.
 SUMBA CABIN
 The Sumba cabin immerses you in the culture and natural beauty of Sumba. This guest cabin measures 4 meters by 2.8 meters, providing a cozy yet inviting space. Original art from the island decorates the cabin, creating a unique atmosphere. The queen-size bed ensures a comfortable stay for two adult passengers, as you explore the cultural and natural wonders of Indonesia.
 BORNEO CABIN
 The Borneo cabin measures 4 meters by 2.8 meters, creating an intimate and comfortable space. Adorned with original art reflecting Borneo's tribal traditions, it features a queen-size bed designed for two adult passengers. Experience the comfort and cultural richness of Borneo within this meticulously appointed cabin
 Note: Silolona Sojourns can arrange child extra beds in all cabins, ensuring that families can embark on this extraordinary journey together. Each cabin, with its generous dimensions, is thoughtfully designed to allow you to immerse yourself in the beauty of Indonesia while enjoying the utmost comfort and cultural richness.
