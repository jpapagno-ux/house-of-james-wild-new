Safari Suite The Duba Plains Suite is raised on recycled railway sleeper decking and offers guests fabulous views and a steady stream of wildlife parading past. This private 2-bedroomed Suite has a wrap-around private verandah complete with two private plunge pools and shaded outdoor seating areas. In addition, the Duba Plains Suite comes complete with the services of a personal butler, chef, private safari vehicle, and wildlife guide. This is the perfect private safari camp partner to the 2-bedroomed Dhow Suite at Zarafa or Selinda Suite , both located in the private Selinda Reserve. Read More The heart of the Suite is an expansive indoor lounge and dining area. Each bedroom, which flank the central lounge and dining area, offers guests beautifully appointed en-suite facilities, including indoor and outdoor showers and a luxurious bath. Fans and a solar-powered air-conditioning system over the guest’s bed area ensure that the tent’s design maintains a comfortable climate. The Duba Plains Suite offers a professional camera and a selection of lenses for your complimentary use. At the end of your safari stay with us, your wildlife images and video footage is downloaded as our gift to you to take home. High-quality binoculars are available for you to use while with us at Duba Plains Suite. Safari Experiences The private Duba Concession prides itself on offering extraordinary luxury safari wildlife experiences in Botswana. Many consider this region Botswana’s Okavango Delta’s ‘Maasai Mara’ because of the sheer volume of wildlife. Read More The Duba Plains Suite guests automatically enjoy the services of a private guide and wildlife-viewing safari vehicle. Here, you will enjoy intimate and up-close wildlife viewing opportunities. Wildlife often seen includes lions, leopards, elephants, lechwe, buffalo and everything in between. Duba Plains is also known for occasional sights of interesting Kalahari species like aardwolves and pangolins. Wildlife viewing activities concentrate on exploring this private concession in open safari vehicles, guided walks, traditional canoes (mokoros) and boating. Begin Your Journey Lasting Impact Great Plains Female Rangers: Empowering Women on the Front Line of Conservation The Female Ranger Program invites women from local communities to embark on a 12-month training program equipping them with a plethora of skills, including data collection, first aid, computer skills, driving skills, and more. They are then hired and deployed in our concessions to monitor and manage the biodiversity. Great Plains’ Female Rangers also offers opportunities to shift gender stereotypes, empower vulnerable women, and establish strong female role models for young girls. The long-term conservation impact of this project is equally powerful; female rangers will help foster the community buy-in necessary to preserve Africa’s most precious biodiversity.
