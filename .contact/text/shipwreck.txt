Surrounded by shifting dunes, Atlantic fog, and a rich maritime history, the lodge is thoughtfully positioned in one of the most remote corners of the country. The architecture is inspired by the shipwrecks scattered along the coast, with cabin-style structures shaped like stranded vessels, blending seamlessly into the surrounding dunes and rugged landscape. Here, understated luxury and genuine hospitality create a comfortable base from which to explore a coastline shaped by nature, time, and legend.
