 Silolona Sojourns People
 Embark on a journey like no other with Silolona Sojourns, where sailing becomes an art, and exploration knows no bounds. Our crew is a finely-tuned orchestra of maritime experts and passionate adventurers who are dedicated to crafting unforgettable experiences on the open sea. Each member brings their unique expertise to the table, ensuring your voyage is not just a trip, but a masterpiece of nautical adventure. 
 Ikrar
 Operations Manager
 Ikrar
 Operations Manager
 Ikrar has been an integral part of Silolona Sojourns for over 15 years. He began his journey as an IT specialist and has since evolved into a key figure overseeing 360-degree boat operations. Serving as the vital liaison between our shore office and onboard crews, Ikrar ensures seamless coordination behind the scenes. His meticulous planning and close collaboration with the captains and crew are what make each voyage run effortlessly—so our guests can simply relax and enjoy the journey.
 Kamto
 Captain 
 Kamto
 Captain 
 Hailing from East Java, Captain Kamto's return to Silolona infuses the voyage with his extensive knowledge of Indonesian waters. With years of experience, he charts a course through the archipelago's intricate nuances, accompanied by our dedicated crew of chefs, stewards, engineers, and deckhands. His leadership, supported by the yacht's expedition leader, PADI dive instructor, bosun, and our professional Bali-based office team, ensures an expedition that is both thrilling and secure.
 Bambang
 Captain
 Bambang
 Captain
 East Java's Captain Bambang stands at the helm as a seasoned professional, leading our crew with confidence. Beyond his maritime expertise, he carries a culinary prowess that adds a flavorful dimension to our voyages. His mastery of creating the perfect sambal, a spicy Indonesian condiment, has earned him a respected reputation among the crew. With Captain Bambang's guidance, you'll embark on remarkable journeys with the assurance of capable hands.
 Nasir
 Head Carpenter / Builder of Silolona
 Nasir
 Head Carpenter / Builder of Silolona
 Meet Nasir, originated from Ara, Sulawesi, the craftsman behind the transformation of Silolona from ancient tree trunks to a magnificent Phinisi. His artistry and dedication are palpable in every corner of the ship. A true force of nature, Nasir chose to accompany Silolona on her voyages, embodying the spirit of adventure that defines us.
 Goris Atawuwur
 Cruise Director
 Goris Atawuwur
 Cruise Director
 Guests are often first welcomed by Goris’ warm and unmistakable smile—only to soon discover the remarkable depth of experience behind it. Having spent decades as a dive instructor and explorer of Indonesia’s waters, he carries an intimate knowledge of marine life and hidden corners of the archipelago.For over 30 years, Goris journeyed alongside Silolona founder Patti Seery, helping shape the expeditions that define Silolona Sojourns today. As a Trip Leader, he shares not only destinations, but stories, insights, and a profound connection to the sea.Part of the original Silolona building team, Goris is woven into the very soul of the vessel itself. When not guiding guests, he often pursues another passion—photography—capturing fleeting moments of beauty above and below the waterline.
 Alberto Rodrigues Puentez
 Cruise Director
 Alberto Rodrigues Puentez
 Cruise Director
 With nearly 10 years of life in Indonesia and more than 15 years of worldwide diving and liveaboard experience, Alberto enriches Silolona Sojourns with both expertise and artistry. Fluent in English and Spanish, he leads expeditions with professionalism and warmth, connecting effortlessly with an international clientele. An artist at heart, Alberto brings a creative dimension to every voyage. His passion for capturing the delicate details of the underwater world through drawing transforms dives into lasting works of memory—an experience that resonates deeply with guests who share his love for the ocean.
 Ketut Kusumawardana
 Chef
 Ketut Kusumawardana
 Chef
 Meet Ketut, the amiable chef from picturesque Buleleng, Bali, known for his warm hospitality and perpetual smile. As the maestro of desserts, Ketut enchants taste buds with sweet symphonies, showcasing his culinary expertise in decadent cakes and intricate pastries—a testament to his mastery in the art of dessert making. Ketut's desserts go beyond palatable delights; they are visual feasts, reflecting meticulous attention to detail and a passion for creating memorable culinary experiences. Whether it's a velvety chocolate mousse or a delicate fruit tart, each creation transports you to a world of sugary bliss. Beyond his culinary skills, Ketut's friendly demeanor adds an extra layer of charm, making each bite a delightful journey into the warm spirit of Balinese hospitality.
 Andi Putrawansyah
 Chef
 Andi Putrawansyah
 Chef
 Andi, despite his youthfulness, enjoys cooking traditional Indonesian and Asian dishes, whilst exploring classical and modern international cuisine. He has rapidly absorbed the methods to produce outstanding presentations which make even the most discerning palate crave more. Andi joined the team over five years ago and being promoted to head chef becoming an integral part of the Silolona Sojourns as his culinary journey continues. He entertains our guests not only with his food, but with his musical talents of singing and soulful guitar.
 I Gede Widiantara

 I Gede Widiantara
 Chief Purser
 For more than three years, Yande has enriched Silolona Sojourns with his experience in hospitality and bar service. His professionalism and refined service skills elevate each moment on board, from crafted beverages to attentive guest care. Known for his keen eye for detail, Yande ensures that every guest feels welcomed, comfortable, and well looked after—an essential part of the Silolona experience.

 Chief Engineer
