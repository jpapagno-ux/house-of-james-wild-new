Watch Video Play Video Fondly referred to as “a place of legends” the camp exudes a laid-back pace that evokes a sense of nostalgia, typical of the ‘pioneer safari’ days. MalaMala Camp is a welcoming home-from-home, where rare photographs, locally-produced wares, old maps and mementos sit comfortably with the works of renowned contemporary wildlife artists. Location MalaMala Game Reserve Greater Kruger South Africa Highlights Legendary, world-renowned destination Panoramic river views throughout Private sanctuary, personalised service Set in centuries-old forests Endless procession of wild animals Camp Details The Suites All thatched, there are eleven suites and eight large rooms styled in rich, earthy tones blend harmoniously into the landscape. Each has unhindered views, either facing the watering hole or overlooking the water-rich flood plains beyond the massive trees that provide shade and shelter to a number of birds and mammals. From the sprawling deck canopied by an enormous Jackal-berry tree, guests have up-close visuals of the passing parade of visitors attracted by the riverine habitat. It’s a good idea to keep your binoculars and camera close at hand when you spend time in this magical spot to capture the element of surprise. Features Earthy comfort, contemporary conveniences Private suites and rooms Pool & gym in prime viewing location Personal mini-bar and coffee station 1 / 10 Next Prev Guest Area The air-conditioned library is the place to take refuge from the heat of the day and relax with a good book or catch up with current affairs on social media. Guests gather in the airy lounge and “Buffalo Bar” in the evenings, which doubles as a “gallery” of fascinating historic photographs depicting the founders and lifestyle of the early pioneer era, which makes for good talking points and discussion. Cuisine With a varied combination of delicious global favourites and Pan-African dishes on offer throughout the day, meals are skilfully paired with a surprising variety of top wines from our cellar. Delicious dinners are served on the spacious deck, around the fire in the traditional reed “boma’ or the air-conditioned dining-room. Pre game drive tea-time with a variety of freshly baked traditional tarts, cakes and snacks on offer is a firm favourite with guests. Boutique & Relaxation Locally made artefacts, jewellery and stylish clothes are found in the Safari boutique. Keep your fitness levels in check with a visit to the small gym facility and massage service if you choose a relaxing pamper. A short walk down the deck’s stairs takes you to a seductive pool and small gym overlooking the floodplains, so you can watch the unfolding action as you get fit! This is one of the few safari destinations with a universally accessible suite, to meet the needs of guests with physical challenges. It is designed in accordance with the Disabled People’s Information Service of South Africa, in line with international standards. Game drives in specially equipped Land Rovers enhance the experiences of those requiring assistance. 1 / 10 Next Prev Info Useful Information Health Guests travelling to the Greater Kruger region are encouraged to consult their physician or travel clinic prior to departure to discuss recommended vaccinations and malaria prevention. While the area is considered low risk, simple precautions such as using insect repellent and wearing long sleeves in the evening are advised. Guests should also travel with any required personal medications and ensure appropriate travel insurance is in place. The lodge team is always available to assist should any medical needs arise during your stay. Weather The MalaMala Game Reserve and the surrounding Greater Kruger National Park enjoy a warm, inviting climate year-round, making this region an exceptional safari destination in every season. Summer (October – April) brings warm days, often in the high-20s to mid-30s °C, with dramatic afternoon thunderstorms that refresh the bush and awaken vibrant birdlife, while many animals give birth during this lush, green season. Winter (May – September) offers crisp mornings and mild, sunlit days, with very little rainfall and more open vegetation, allowing for excellent visibility. Wildlife often gathers around the Sand River and nearby water sources, making game viewing particularly rewarding during the dry winter months. Getting there By Air Guests can fly directly to MalaMala’s Private Airstrip with Federal Air, from either Johannesburg or Kruger Mpumalanga International. Private charter flights to this airstrip are also available. This will be followed by a short 15-minute road transfer to the lodge. Alternatively, guests can fly from Johannesburg or Cape Town into Skukuza Airport, situated within the Kruger National Park, followed by a 60-minute road transfer to the lodge. By Road MalaMala Game Reserve is approximately a 5–6 hour drive from Johannesburg. The journey follows well-maintained national and regional roads through the scenic Lowveld to the town of Hazyview, before entering the Sabi Sand Nature Reserve via Shaw’s Gate and continuing on to MalaMala. For further details and personalised arrangements, kindly contact the concierge team. Children MalaMala Camp welcomes children of all ages, with specific policies for pricing, accommodation and activities. Children aged 0–11 receive a discount when sharing with full-paying adults. While children under 5 are not permitted on standard game drives, or to dine in the boma in the evenings, babysitters can be arranged for a nominal fee. Pre-teens are not allowed on bush walks.
