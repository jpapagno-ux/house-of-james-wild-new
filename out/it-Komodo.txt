A WORLD 
HERITAGE SITE
Covering almost 2,000km2, the 
breathtaking Komodo National Park  
is a UNESCO World Heritage site  
made up of 3 larger islands (Komodo, 
Padar and Rinca) and 26 smaller ones, 
many of which are uninhabited. 
These islands offer superb opportunities 
for hiking and wildlife spotting including 
the native and iconic Komodo dragons.

===== PAGE 3 =====
AN ISLAND  
OF CONTRASTS
Depending on the season, the  
terrain varies from desert savannah 
landscape to lush greenery – both  
are equally arresting. 
Offshore, the park’s rich marine 
ecosystem (which includes 1,000  
species of fish and 260 types of 
coral), affords unforgettable undersea 
adventures for divers and snorkellers  
that are on par with Raja Ampat. 
Dive enthusiasts have hailed  
Komodo as one of the world’s top  
10 diving hotspots.

DAY 1 
Where we go
Labuan Bajo port to Sebayor Island.
What you’ll do
Quickly relax into the Rascal way of life – where no request is 
too great, and no detail too small. As soon as we up-anchor, 
the stresses and strains of everyday life will become a distant 
memory. Spend the afternoon snorkelling, swimming, stand-up 
paddleboarding, diving or simply relaxing on board.
What you’ll see
A wealth of wildlife including white-spotted eagle rays –  
some weighing more than 200kg – gliding through the  
crystal-clear waters or jumping above the waterline.
The highlight
The stunning views of Sangeang Volcano from the  
yacht’s panoramic roof deck while you sip on one of our 
signature sundowners.
Try your hand at
Wakeboarding at thrilling speed whilst being pulled by one  
of our tenders.

DAY 2
Where we go
Off-grid Sape Bay in Sumbawa.
What you’ll do
Getting the sand between your toes on the startling pink 
beaches of Sumbawa – or, alternatively, paint them from an  
easel on the panoramic roof deck; explore Sape Bay by kayak,  
or snorkel and dive its magnificent, secluded reefs.
What you’ll see
Long-tailed macaques, baby blacktip reef sharks and  
blue-spotted ribbontail rays.
The highlight
Enjoy your very own private pop-up beach club with sun 
loungers and parasols set up on the sand where we’ll serve  
you a fabulous picnic-style lunch with ice-cold beers, wines, 
cocktails and mocktails. 
Try your hand at
A wonderful experience for all the family: learn how to  
navigate the old-fashioned way with maps then take  
supervised control of the ship’s wheel as we sail through  
the islands of Indonesia.

===== PAGE 7 =====
DAY 3 
Where we go
Loh Liang in Komodo Island, Siaba Besar (also known as  
Turtle Paradise), and Gili Lawa Darat.
What you’ll do
Take a safari on foot around Komodo Island to search for its 
famous dragons – who may be lying in the sun or out stalking 
their prey.
What you’ll see
Alongside Komodo dragons, there are water buffaloes, Timor 
deer and wild boars as well as a vast array of birdlife including 
sunbirds, orioles, kingfishers and yellow-crested cockatoos.
The highlight
Drifting in the water and getting up close to hawksbill and  
giant green turtles.
Try your hand at
Learn how to create Rascal’s world-famous spicy sambal  
(a classic Indonesian accompaniment to barbecued dishes), 
made with kecap manis (sweet soy sauce), red chilli, tomato, 
shallots and lime.

===== PAGE 8 =====
DAY 4
where we go
Taka Makassar, Tatawa Besar and Seraya Islands.
What you’ll do
Snorkel and dive with manta rays; or take a walk along  
Taka Makassar – a perfect crescent of white sand set in the 
middle of brilliant turquoise waters, widely considered one  
of the most beautiful swimming spots in the world.
What you’ll see
The giant trevally – a type of kingfish which can reach up to  
5.5ft (1.6m) in length, and the blacktip sharks of Anita’s Island 
which are not an aggressive species but will approach divers  
and snorkellers out of curiosity.
The highlight
Rascal’s magical pop-up restaurant: come nightfall, you’ll be 
ferried by tender to a secret beach festooned with lanterns, 
bamboo and palm-leaf decorations for our signature dinner 
under the stars.
Try your hand at
Join our crew who’ll teach you some Indonesian songs and 
dance moves as they serenade you after dinner.

EXTEND YOUR VOYAGE
Explore beyond Komodo with these exciting extensions:
+1 night
Visit Komodo Island Village to meet the welcoming Komodo 
and Bima communities. End the day with a thrilling night dive, 
spotting nocturnal predators like moray eels.
+2 nights
Snorkel or dive at Gili Banta, a marine wonder where Pacific and 
Indian Ocean currents converge. And explore South Komodo, 
home to stunning soft coral reefs and the iconic Manta Alley.
+3 nights
Discover Sangeang Volcano, an active peak with unique marine 
life like pygmy seahorses. Enjoy diving through bubble-filled 
reefs and relax on black-sand beaches with chance sightings of 
buffalo and more.
+4 nights 
Follow the Ring of Fire route to Sumbawa Besar or Bali,  
where you can swim with majestic whale sharks and visit the 
enchanting Mata Jitu Waterfall on Moyo Island.

GETTING THERE
All voyages start and end in the port of Labuan Bajo on  
the island of Flores. There are daily flights from Denpasar, 
Bali (1 hour) and Jakarta (2 hours) to Labuan Bajo airport.
The port where you’ll embark is just a short 10-minute  
drive from the airport. Our crew will collect you from the 
airport and/or your hotel/villa accommodation on Flores. 
The voyage begins at 11am on the first day and  
ends at 1pm on the final day, so flights should be 
scheduled accordingly.  
If you require assistance with flight schedules, flight 
bookings (including both commercial and private)  
and itinerary extensions, please don’t hesitate to ask  
our friendly team who will be more than happy to make 
your travel plans as seamless as possible.
