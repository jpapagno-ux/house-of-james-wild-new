in the world.

DAY 1 
Where we go
Babi and Pomana Islands.
What you’ll do
Slip into the Rascal way of life before a first snorkel or dive: 
Pomana has a beautiful shallow reef dropping to deep walls,  
all with spectacular coral growth; while Babi is home to  
‘The Crack’, a 30m-long reef fissure that is home to a vast array  
of aquatic life.
What you’ll see
Bumphead parrotfish, reef sharks, eagle rays, hawksbill turtles 
and spider crabs.
The highlight
A sunset beach stroll before a gourmet dinner on board 
prepared by one of our two private chefs.
Try your hand at
Creating your very own cocktail or mocktail with the help  
of the Rascal bartender.

===== PAGE 5 =====
DAY 2
Where we go
Pangabatan Island, with its gin-clear waters and  
white-sand beaches.
What you’ll do
Spend the day relaxing; snorkel or dive the surrounding  
coral reefs; then set sail for Serbete Island for a chance to  
spot whales en route.
What you’ll see
Huge gorgonian sea fans, black coral bushes, whitetip reef 
sharks, octopus, napoleon wrasse and eagle rays. 
The highlight
Enjoying your very own private beach club complete with  
pop-up bar, parasols, sun loungers, ice-cold towels and a 
gourmet picnic lunch. 
Try your hand at
Exploring the mangroves by paddleboard or kayak; for the  
more adventurous, try and break the Rascal record of holding  
on to the flying mattress as the tender whips you around at  
full speed.

===== PAGE 6 =====
DAY 3 
Where we go
Serbete and Adonara Islands.
What you’ll do
Start the day off Serbete Island, snorkelling and diving its shallow 
reef plateaus with impressive drop-offs before joining the dozens 
of reef sharks at the famous 24m-deep ‘shark plateau’; continue 
your voyage to Adonara Island, home to a wide variety of corals, 
and perfect for more exciting reef, slope and ledge dives.
What you’ll see
Schools of fusiliers, juvenile harlequin sweetlips, whitetip and 
grey reef sharks, hawksbill turtles, marbled stingrays, eagle rays 
and clown triggerfish.
The highlight
Our signature pop-up dinner under the stars with music  
(and dancing) afterwards, courtesy of the Rascal crew band.
Try your hand at
Improving your underwater photography skills trying to capture 
the reef sharks of ‘shark plateau’.

DAY 4
where we go
The island of Kawula, home to the most dramatic volcanic 
scenery in the Eastern Lesser Sunda region.
What you’ll do
Trek inland on a birdwatching safari to see lesser frigate birds 
and white-bellied sea eagles; discover the incredible snorkel and 
dive sites where volcanic vents gush hot and cold water into the 
ocean, attracting a wealth of marine life.
What you’ll see
White and blacktip reef sharks, tuna, sea turtles, giant trevallies 
and schooling barracudas.
The highlight
Waking up to the incredible views of a smoking volcano and  
a shoreline fringed with black-sand beaches.
Try your hand at
Capturing the panorama in watercolours; for the more active, 
become a true Rascal by jumping off the 7m-high roof deck.

DAY 5
Where we go
North of Alor and Buaya Islands – where the waters of the  
Sawu and Banda Seas meet.
What you’ll do
On land: visit the village of Takpala and meet the Abui people. 
Underwater: explore the stunning reefs that make this one of  
the top snorkel and dive destinations in Indonesia.
What you’ll see
Below the waves: an extraordinary abundance of marine life 
which few travellers get to witness in their lifetime. On shore:  
the fascinating culture of the Abui who live in traditional 
redwood bamboo houses called Fala Foka. 
The highlight
Experiencing the traditions of the Abui people who call the rains 
with the moko drum, and who use betel nuts to communicate 
with their ancestors and to keep away evil spirits.
Try your hand at
Join in the traditional Lego-Lego dance, which symbolises unity, 
and is performed at ceremonies and to welcome guests.

DAY 6
where we go
Buaya (also known as Crocodile Island for its distinctive shape) 
and Pura Island, which rises 1,015m up to its highest point:  
the rim of a volcanic cone.
What you’ll do
Drift dive or snorkel at the famous sites of ‘Red Wall’ and  
‘Clown Valley’, home to some of the most biodiverse marine  
life in the archipelago; free dive with the local children of  
Pak Yan village on Pura Island.
What you’ll see
Schools of anthias, pipefish and the spectacular ray-finned 
rhinopias scorpionfish. With the current in our favour, we’ll 
hopefully encounter Mola Mola and thresher sharks too.
The highlight
Vast fields of multi-coloured anemones – one of the greatest 
concentrations in the world – that cover the shallow reefs of 
‘Clown Valley’.
Try your hand at
Buy a handmade sarong on Buaya Island (famed for its textiles), 
and wear it like a local to dinner.

EXTEND YOUR VOYAGE
We recommend a minimum of 6 nights to discover the  
Eastern Lesser Sunda Islands, but they easily can be explored 
for longer for those seeking more land-based or underwater 
adventures – or more time to relax in between activities. 
+1 night
Head to Wetar Island, whose rugged mountains are covered 
with tropical rainforest and whose waters are home to superb 
snorkel and dive spots; meet “Mawar”, a wild male dugong who 
is famously very friendly and enjoys playing in a sheltered bay  
off Alor Island.
+2 nights
Visit Wetar Island and the uninhabited Reong in the  
so-called ‘Forgotten Islands’ territory of Indonesia.  
The mangrove-covered shoreline is perfect for exploration  
by kayak or stand-up paddleboard; or take a tender tour to  
go in search of saltwater crocodiles.
+3 nightS
Start your voyage from Komodo Island, where alongside superb 
snorkelling and diving, you can go in search of the iconic 
Komodo dragons; en route to Maumere, explore the magnificent 
reefs off the coast of Paluweh, a smoking volcanic island 
featuring lava domes and a cone that rises 875m above sea level.

GETTING THERE
The voyage commences in Maumere and ends in Alor;  
the itinerary can be arranged in either direction. 
There are multiple weekly flights from Jakarta and Bali 
to Maumere Airport and Mali Airport for Alor. Flights to/
from Maumere require a connection in either Labuan Bajo 
or Kupang. Flights to/from Alor require a connection in 
Kupang. The port of Maumere is a short 15-minute drive 
from Mali Airport; the port of Alor is a 20-minute drive from 
Mali Airport.
Our crew will collect you from the airport or your hotel. The 
voyage begins at 11am on the first day and ends at 1pm on 
the final day and flights should be scheduled accordingly.
If you require assistance with flight schedules, flight 
bookings (including both commercial and private), and 
itinerary extensions, please don’t hesitate to ask our 
friendly team who will be more than happy to make your 
travel plans as seamless as possible.
