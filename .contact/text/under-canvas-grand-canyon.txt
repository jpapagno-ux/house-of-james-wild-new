Plan Your Adventure Location Directions Map DIRECTIONS & TRAVEL INFO Directions from Valle, Arizona (via Hwy 64) Turn into the Valle Airport entrance off Hwy 64. Follow the road (and the white Under Canvas signs) for 2 miles around the side of the airport. You will make two 90 degree turns and the last 0.7 miles will be unpaved. You will pass through one metal gate. Turn right into our driveway. If you pass through a second metal gate you have gone too far. Follow our driveway to our large lobby tent. *Some GPS maps label us in Williams, AZ. We are located at Grand Canyon Junction (Valle, AZ) which is 28 miles north of Williams, AZ. Directions from Phoenix, Arizona Take I-17 North and then I-40 West to Williams, approximately 117 miles. Then take AZ-64 North for 28 miles, or approximately half an hour, until you get to Grand Canyon Junction/Valle, AZ. Then follow above. Under Canvas Grand Canyon is approximately 3.5 hours from Phoenix, AZ. Find Us On Google Grand Canyon FAQs What is the closest airport to the Grand Canyon? The closest airport to Under Canvas Grand Canyon is Flagstaff Pulliam Airport , Flagstaff, AZ (1 hour), Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport , Phoenix, AZ (3 hours),or Harry Reid International Airport , Las Vegas, NV (3.5 hours). When is the best time to visit the Grand Canyon? The best time to visit Grand Canyon National Park is April through October, when the weather is comfortable. Because the Grand Canyon is higher in elevation, temperatures remain quite comfortable through the summer. Are dogs allowed in the Grand Canyon? Pets are allowed in developed areas such as: in your cars while driving park roads, picnic areas, along roads when you are stopped, in parking areas, and in boats on lakes where motorized watercraft are permitted. Dogs are welcome to stay at Under Canvas Grand Canyon! Check out our blog on things to do with your dog around the Grand Canyon. How far is Lake Powell from the Grand Canyon? Lake Powell is 154 miles or a 2-and-a-half-hour drive from Grand Canyon’s South Rim. Is EV charging available on site at Under Canvas Grand Canyon? Under Canvas Grand Canyon does not currently offer on-site EV charging. The nearest option is a ChargePoint station just a few minutes down AZ-64 in Valle. For more options, including a Tesla Supercharger , head to Williams, AZ, about 30 minutes from camp.
