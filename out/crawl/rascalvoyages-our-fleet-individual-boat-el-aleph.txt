 El Aleph
 El Aleph is a traditional handmade Phinisi yacht built in 2008 in Serangan, Indonesia. Measuring 39m in length, she accommodates up to 10 guests across five cabins and features generous indoor and outdoor living areas, including an expansive main deck for sunbathing by day and alfresco dining by night. Refurbished by Rascal Voyages in 2025, her interiors have been reimagined with lighter teak, curated antiques, and handwoven textures, blending timeless Indonesian heritage with Rascal’s signature Archipelago Chic aesthetic.
