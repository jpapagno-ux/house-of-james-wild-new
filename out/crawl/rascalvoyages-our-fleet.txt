 OUR SAILING FOOTPRINT
 All our cabins feature organic, biodegradable and reef-safe toiletries. All single-use plastics have been removed and replaced with natural materials such as wood and glass. At an operational level, we’re one of the few fleets in Indonesia with eco-friendly sewage treatment units onboard. Our ships’ waste oil is captured by our oil-water separators and is recorded and delivered to marine authorities in each harbour of operation. Finally, we are rolling out noise-cancelling boxes on all our fleet’s engines so as not to disturb the wildlife we are privileged to share our journey with as we sail through the islands of Indonesia.
