Book Your Country House in the City Proudly awarded One MICHELIN Key, No. 1 Pery Square stands among the finest hotels in Ireland — a recognition of our exceptional hospitality, attention to detail, and unique character. Overlooking the handsome, tree-lined People’s Park in Limerick’s historic Georgian Quarter, No. 1 Pery Square is a luxurious and intimate boutique townhouse hotel. With 20 individually styled bedrooms, delicious food and wine served in our restaurant and lounges, an innovative and polished organic spa in the vaulted basement, a sweet terrace and garden room, the hotel is full of personality, comfort, and style throughout. Located in the heart of the Georgian Quarter, one of Limerick’s most architecturally rich and culturally vibrant neighbourhoods, No. 1 is your elegant country house in the city — offering all the charm of a heritage property, with the warmth of personal hospitality. No. 1 is also one of the country’s prettiest and most characterful hotels. The vision of owner Patricia Roberts, who purchased the building in 2006, can be felt throughout. Part of a terrace of six Georgian houses built around 1830 by the Pery Square Tontine Company, the hotel lovingly preserves the spirit of the past while embracing the best of modern comforts.
