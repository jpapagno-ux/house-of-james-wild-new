 A Journey through our Cabins 
 Indulge in Indonesian Elegance
 Authentic colors, textures, and styles derived from the cultures they embody endow Si Datu Bua with a relaxed yet distinctive atmosphere. Spacious bathing facilities are tastefully integrated into the decor providing in a gracious well considered manner those amenities so essential to a comfortable standard of living. 
 Indulge in the lap of luxury as you step into the meticulously appointed cabins of Datu Bua, each a spacious dwelling unique unto itself, adorned with original art from the cultures of the Indonesia. Every moment spent in these opulent sanctuaries is an experience that defines the essence of a luxury yacht charter in Indonesia.

 BANDA CABIN
 The Banda Suite (6.8m x 5.6m) is our master cabin, a sanctuary fit for royalty. Here, you'll find a king-size bed, perfectly cocooned in luxury, ready to cradle you into a world of comfort. Ideal for two adult passengers, it offers the epitome of opulence and tranquility. 
 TORAJA CABIN
 The Toradja cabin (5.8m x 4.1m) boasts identical elegance and comfort. It features a king-size bed, providing an exquisite haven for two adult passengers. Immerse yourself in luxury and let the soothing ambiance of Toraja embrace you. 
 CELEBES CABIN
 Celebes, like its namesake, offers a spacious layout (5.8m x 4.1m) and panoramic views that invite you to savor the beauty of Indonesia from the comfort of your own oasis. Adorned with a king-size bed, this cabin is a celebration of relaxation and artistry, offering an enchanting retreat for two adult passengers 
 Note: Child extra beds can be arranged in all cabins, ensuring a comfortable and family-friendly experience aboard Si Datu Bua.
