 ESCAPE TO KOMODO 
 KOMODO, RASCAL-STYLE
 Take a guided walking safari as we go in search of flying foxes, sulphur-crested cockatoos and Komodo dragons; feel the sand between your toes on the pink beach of Pantai Pulau Lambu; explore Manta Point, a rich feeding ground for manta rays, some of which reach up to 5 metres wide; and enjoy one of our signature sundowners on the panoramic roof deck—complete with spectacular views of the soaring Sangeang volcano.
 WILDLIFE HIGHLIGHT: 
HERE BE DRAGONS
 The Komodo dragon is the largest living lizard on earth—with fully grown adults reaching up to 10 feet (3 metres) in length and weighing more than 150 pounds (70 kg). Endemic to the Indonesian islands of Komodo, Rinca, Flores and Gili Motang, we may spot this fearsome apex predator, either resting in the shade or stalking their prey; the thrill of seeing them in the wild is just one of many highlights of our voyage to wildlife-abundant Komodo National Park.       
 SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE: DINNER UNDER THE STARS
 This is one of our favourite surprises to arrange for our guests. While you're busy with the day's activities, we set up the Rascal pop-up restaurant on a secret, jungle-fringed, white-sand beach. Draped with traditional bamboo and palm-leaf decorations, here our chefs will serve you lobster, wagyu beef, and satay fresh from the grill. On a clear night there'll be a magnificent view of the Milky Way; you may even see bioluminescent plankton glowing blue-green in the water as we tender you back to the boat at bedtime.

 RAJA AMPAT
 Raja Ampat is quite simply one of the world’s most beautiful archipelagos—comprising over 1,500 islands, its spectacular scenery includes fine white-sand beaches, hidden lagoons and surreally shaped limestone islets swathed in jungle. An unrivalled site for diving and snorkelling, it is home to a greater variety of wildlife than any other coastal region on Earth, including 75% of all the known corals, 17 different marine mammals, and more than 1,600 species of fish.
