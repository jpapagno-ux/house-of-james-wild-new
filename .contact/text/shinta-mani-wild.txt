JAN – FEB CAMPFIRES & BUTTERFLIES There is no bad time to visit Shinta Mani Wild — it’s just as fabulous in winter, when the butterflies are plentiful, as in summer, when the thunder of raging waterfalls competes with the hoots of mating gibbons. location The Khmer New Year sees low water levels that allow for heading deep into the rainforest for mountain biking and hiking. Over this season, the trickle in the river rises to 1/2 meter (20 inches), and we are grateful for the odd 15-minute showers that cool and refresh the jungle valley. mar – may SHALLOW WATERS, DEEP EXPLORATIONS When the rain, mist, and monsoons begin their dance, our 17 intermittent rivers and streams come back to life and you may witness a torrential outpouring. Trees begin to fruit — attracting the birds — and our favorite herd of wild elephants sometimes makes an appearance. The Tmor Rung River has ample water for kayaking and bird watching: The many drongos and kingfishers are just delightful. It’s a great time for foraging as well. jun – july MISTY MOUNTAINTOPS & FORAGING The pinnacle of the wet season is when you’ll experience the jungle at its most glorious. Our three major waterfalls bulk up tenfold at least, with Raging Big Sister Falls producing a veritable mountain of mist. The pileated gibbons are especially vocal in the early mornings this time of year, their calls to establish territorial boundaries seemingly louder and more serious. The orchids are perhaps at their most glorious toward the end of this season. aug – oct WHEN WATER FALLS & ORCHIDS BLOOM As the orchids remain in bloom, the cry of the Raging Sisters calms and a great season for swimming in the river begins. At Srey Ambel estuary, remote sandy beaches reveal themselves for picnics and sun worshippers. This is also the beginning of the butterfly season. nov – dec PICNICS & RIVER SWIMS A REAL IMPACT Our partnership with Wildlife Alliance supports a team of rangers who patrol the Cardamom Forest for poaching, logging, and other illegal activity. ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCE HORSEBACK RIDING Ride a Mustang horse alongside a local and experience the rhythm and lifestyle of the kingdom that shares its name. These compact yet robust ponies, renowned for their strength and gentle demeanor, have been pivotal to the Himalayan way of life for centuries. Before the modern era, these noble steeds were the lifelines of the region, facilitating travel and trade across the challenging terrain. Our horseback expeditions range from short, leisurely rides to neighboring villages — perfect for beginners — to more adventurous multi-day excursions into the rugged terrain of Upper Mustang. Designed for experienced riders, these longer itineraries offer an unparalleled insight into the nomadic lifestyle, as old and untamed as the mountains themselves. additional EXPERIENCE HIKING & TREKKING Mustang’s contrasting landscapes encompass arid deserts, snowy peaks, colorful valleys, and mountain forests. CORE EXPERIENCE WELLNESS THERAPIES After experiencing the cultural and scenic adventures of Mustang, a tailor-made wellness regimen will soothe your body and relax your spirit. As part of your all-inclusive experience at Shinta Mani Mustang, you will enjoy a personalized program in our stylish spa overlooking the Nilgiri range. The experience begins with a consultation with our local Amchi (Tibetan medicine doctor), Tsewang Gyurme Gurung, the 11th-generation recorded Amchi in his lineage of SoRig practitioners. He will review your overall wellness and prescribe specific treatments, drawing from a wealth of ancestral knowledge. Our spa facilities offer private treatment rooms for individuals and couples, steam and sauna rooms, and massage and beauty treatments with highly-trained therapists. download spa menu CORE EXPERIENCE LUBRA, BON BUDDHIST VILLAGE An extraordinary fusion of nature, culture, and spirituality, Lubra Village is an 800-year-old settlement nestled in the heart of Mustang and one of the last remaining strongholds of the Bon Buddhist tradition, a practice that predates Buddhism. This secluded hamlet, accessible via a picturesque hike along the Panda Khola River gorge, is a testament to the resilience of long-held traditions in the face of time. From the venerable gompa and intriguing cave monastery to the local cuisine served on the rooftop of an ancient home, Lubra offers a voyage back in time to the roots of Bon Buddhism, set against the majestic backdrop of the Himalayas. Enjoy a day of trekking through the picturesque landscapes, sampling the unique flavors of traditional Bon cuisine, and gaining a deeper understanding of a way of life that has been virtually unchanged for 800 years. CORE EXPERIENCE JHARKOT, JHONG, MUKTINATH, KAGBENI This tour blends history, culture, adventure, spirituality, and natural beauty for an immersive experience in the Nepalese countryside. Begin after breakfast with a one-hour drive to Jharkot, a tranquil Tibetan village seemingly untouched by time and famous for its historic gompa (monastery). From Jharkot, a serene hike into the valley passes through age-old orchards to the revered pilgrimage site of Muktinath, which draws both Hindu and Buddhist pilgrims from around the world, particularly for the sacred 108 fountains known as Mukti Dhara. According to Hindu belief, bathing in these fountains washes away one’s sins and brings salvation (we recommend bringing your bathing suit to partake in this unique ritual). From Muktinath you will cross suspension bridges and ascend the opposite side of the valley to ancient Jhong village. Here, you’ll visit a traditional home and enjoy a lunch prepared by the Shinta Mani culinary team at the monastery, against the breathtaking backdrop of 360-degree views of the valleys and mountains. After the meal, choose between an exhilarating mountain biking adventure down a less-traveled trail or a comfortable jeep ride towards Kagbeni village, the gateway to Upper Mustang. Here, you’ll visit the Kag Chode Thupten Samphel Ling Monastery, an important Buddhist site, to end this exploration of Mustang’s spiritual and adventurous highlights. CORE EXPERIENCE MARPHA VILLAGE Nestled at the southern edge of Mustang, Marpha Village is a hidden gem famous for its beautifully painted Buddhist monasteries, traditional stone houses, and apple orchards where you can sample the local brandy and cider. This authentic encounter with Nepalese rural life begins with a refreshing, two-hour morning trek from Shinta Mani Wild to Dumba Lake and the iconic Khutsab Terenga Monastery. This route offers transfixing views of the Kali Gandaki Valley, the deepest valley in the world. At the monastery, you’ll be treated to an elegant breakfast picnic under the shade of an old apricot tree. Post-breakfast, you have the option to either drive, trek, or mountain bike to Marpha. Spend your day strolling down the narrow flagstone streets, shopping for local handicrafts, and absorbing the sights and sounds of this picturesque village. Lunch is hosted by Mrs. Kamala, a renowned Thakali chef and civic leader who will prepare a traditional meal for you at her kitchen table, offering an authentic taste of local cuisine. After lunch, delve deeper into the fascinating history and traditions of Marpha Village, from the orchards to the monasteries. CULTURAL DISCOVERIES Pub Street Think of Pub Street as Southeast Asia’s version of Bourbon Street in New Orleans: several lively blocks lined with bars and clubs that entertain revelers all night long. Officially called Street 8 and just a short walk from the hotel, it’s a spirited destination for having a few drinks, sampling authentic Khmer street food, and watching performances by local musicians and dancers. Pro tip: Start with dinner at Elia, a Greek restaurant in an old colonial house (try the watermelon feta salad and the Rhodes sausage), before your bar-hopping adventure. CULTURAL DISCOVERIES Siem Reap’s Coffee Culture Coffee is a way of life in Cambodia, and hip young locals have opened a number of charming cafés across Siem Reap, many of them making drinks made with locally grown and roasted beans. A few of our favorites: The Little Red Fox, in Kandal Village, whose beans are all sourced from Southeast Asia; Kambu, in Wat Bo, an adorable Japanese kissaten-style café; and the Muffin Man, on Sumbai Road, where you can pair your espresso with Dieng Dorm’s scrumptious muffins and cakes. CULTURAL DISCOVERIES Phare Cambodian Circus Uniquely Cambodian and daringly modern, Phare offers Siem Reap’s most authentic and energetic evening entertainment. More than just a circus, Phare performers tell Cambodian stories — historical, folk, and modern — with a fusion of theater, music, dance, and modern circus arts. ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES BIRDWATCHING Hundreds of species of birds live in and around Shinta Mani Wild, and many, many more pass through the preserve during the migratory seasons. Guests can spot six species of kingfishers, 10 different types of drongos, eagles, hornbills, and more. ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES BUTTERFLY ENCOUNTERS Wild is home to over 140 species of butterflies, a kaleidoscope of hundreds of painted Jezebels, magpie crows, and sapphire-colored blue-banded king crows. Head Bensley Butler and lepidopterist Bong Mac will guide you on a hike and share his passion for butterflies with you. At our new butterfly hatchery, developed in partnership with Conservation International, we breed indigenous species for reintroduction into their natural habitat. Immerse yourself in the serene, meditative ambiance while learning about the butterfly's fascinating life cycle — then carry a specially curated box of butterflies to an isolated location within the preserve and set them free into the wild! ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES FORAGING The Cardamom Rainforest is a natural larder, and our wild foragers — most of them reformed poachers and loggers who know how to live off the rainforest — will guide you through the bounty of the jungle. You may dig up wild potatoes, pick exotic fruits off trees, discover edible mushrooms, and taste some unexpected flavors (including, yes, live red ants). ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES CAMERA TRAPPING As part of our conservation agenda, Shinta Mani Wild maintains a network of camera traps that capture footage of our resident species and help scientists keep tabs on the area’s biodiversity. Join our resident naturalist on a nature walk to check the camera traps and gibbon stations. In the past, we’ve found endangered species such as the pileated gibbon, dhole (a type of wild dog), pangolins, and even new species of orchids, macaques, and leopard cats. ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES ZIPLINING Climb Eastern Tower’s 102 steps, strap on a harness, and clip into a double cable for extra safety before flying at upwards of 50 kmph for 400 meters (31mph for 1,300 feet) over the jungle canopy and the river. Catch your breath at the West Tower before one last zip over the face of Raging Big Sister Falls, arriving at the Landing Zone Bar — where your favorite cocktail awaits. ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES / seasonal KAYAKING The Southern Cardamom rainforest has some of the highest annual rainfall in Southeast Asia — and all that water drains to the Gulf of Thailand through a massive system of rivers and estuaries that are perfect for flatwater kayaking. The gentle current will guide you downstream, palm fronds dangling overhead, breeze and birdsong the only sounds. Indeed, this is one of the best ways to birdwatch here, with bee-eaters, black-crested bulbuls, and six different kingfishers all native to the area. NOTE: This experience is dependent upon the water level; during monsoon rains, the river becomes too high to navigate safely. So whether it’s a facial in your tent, a stone foot massage by the river, a yoga session in the forest, or a full-body scrub in the spa itself, our wellness experiences are an essential part of your stay. learn more core experiences anti-poaching patrols The Cardamom Rainforest is one of Asia's most biodiverse places, with populations of rare and endangered wildlife — including Malayan sun bears, pangolins, clouded leopards, and pileated gibbons. But their existence is threatened by poaching, logging, and other illegal activities. That is why we help fund Wildlife Alliance, a Cambodian NGO whose armed rangers patrol the area, seizing snares, running loggers out of their camps, and confiscating guns and chainsaws. Here at Shinta Mani Wild, you are on the front lines of conservation, and your presence alone is helping us fight this fight. Our guests can have the eye-opening experience of joining the rangers on motorbikes for 2-6 hours (depending on your stamina) as they survey and protect the resort’s 350 hectares (865 acres) to ensure the sanctity of this precious habitat. You might help them dismantle a homemade trap — or even rescue and release an animal caught in a snare. learn more core experiences / Seasonal expedition boats Explore the secret backwaters, scenic rice fields, and golden sand beaches of the upper Srey Ambel Estuary on our custom-designed expedition pontoon, equipped with a full bar, daybeds, toilet, and a top deck with sun loungers.
