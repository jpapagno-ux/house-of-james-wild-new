 WE CARE DEEPLY ABOUT OUR DESTINATIONS
 For Rascal, it's a privilege to sail through a region so rich in indigenous cultures and precious marine life. So, alongside ensuring ethical practices in swimming, snorkelling and diving, we participate in scientific surveys and local biodiversity monitoring, and partner up with conservation groups that strive to leave behind a lasting environmental legacy. On land, we extend the hand of genuine friendship to the communities we visit, actively helping to uplift the lives and opportunities of the people who call this part of the world home.
 GOOD FOOD IS THE FOUNDATION OF HAPPINESS
 We design all of Rascal's international and Asian-inspired menus in our beautiful Bali test kitchen, so that on board we can serve you some of the finest and most flavourful food you'll find on the high seas. We'll happily go off-menu, too. Fancy a hamburger on the beach with a glass of champagne? Coming right up! Or feel like an impromptu cooking class? No problem. We'll take you fishing for red snapper with our head chef, then teach you how to slice sashimi, while you sip an ice-cold glass of Chablis.
 WE WELCOME LITTLE RASCALS
 We pride ourselves in creating extraordinary experiences for all the family. For our youngest guests, Rascal's expert guides love to share their passion for exploring the natural world: showing them dolphins and real-life dragons, brilliantly coloured corals and smoking volcanoes. We'll go on treasure hunts together, and set off on exciting educational beach safaris; we'll even teach the little ones how to navigate with maps and supervise them taking the ship's wheel, creating memories for an ocean-going adventure that will last a lifetime.
 THE YACHT IS YOUR HOME ON THE SEAS 
 At Rascal, no request is too great, and no detail is too small. Whether it’s arranging to fill your cabin with your favourite flowers or curating a playlist of special music for a dinner under the stars, we simply love to go the extra nautical mile. Enjoy painting? Then the watercolours will be ready and waiting on the roof deck easel. Passionate about parrots? We’ll design a special birding safari at sunrise to see them. There will be further surprises along the way too, ensuring every itinerary is thoroughly bespoke – and totally unforgettable.
 The Rascal Crew are your extended family. 
 It’s no wonder that many a tear is shed when a Rascal voyage comes to an end. On board, you’ll experience the genuine warmth of our welcome, and share a remarkable journey alongside a team who couldn’t be prouder or happier to show you the very best of Indonesia: the most beautiful beaches; their favourite secret viewpoints; the vibrant indigenous cultures; and some of the continent’s most spectacular wildlife.
