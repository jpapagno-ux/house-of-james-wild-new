Perfectly placed for an immersion into city life, the Jean-Michel Gathy-designed Bangkok hotel is home to 52 suites, a 1,500-square-metre Aman Spa & Wellness centre and several destination dining and socialising venues. Signature Suites A jewel in the hotel's crown, the one-of-a-kind Aman Suite encompasses the entire 18th floor, offering 566 square metres of space, while the Terrace Suite provides 114 square metres, complete with its own open-air terrace. Suites Among the largest in the city, Aman Nai Lert Bangkok's Suites start at 94 square metres boasting floor-to-ceiling windows, with many also enjoying private balconies. View all accommodation Book now Wellbeing A global hub for wellness optimisation The 1,500-square-metre Aman Spa & Wellness centre lies at the heart of Aman Nai Lert Bangkok. Simultaneously steeped in the traditions of Thai healing and – with its Medical Wellness by Hertitude Clinic – at the cutting edge of wellness research, it makes for an unparalleled urban expression of the Aman wellness philosophy. Discover more Dining A new epicurean destination Several dining venues serve as the hotel’s vibrant social hubs. From Arva, Aman's signature Italian concept, to Sesui, a masterful omakase venue and Hiori, an interactive teppanyaki experience, each space further enhances Bangkok’s diverse dining scene, welcoming international visitors and locals alike. Discover more Experiences Uncover Bangkok Whether travelling back in time with a traditional Thai dining experience in the Nai Lert Park Heritage Home or exploring Bangkok’s spiritual treasures at dusk via private tuk-tuk, Aman Nai Lert Bangkok’s bespoke experiences showcase the best of the city, weaving a vibrant tapestry of Thailand’s art, heritage and cuisine. Discover more Getting here Aman Nai Lert Bangkok is located a 45-60 minute drive from Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), Thailand’s main gateway for international and domestic arrivals. Set within the storied Nai Lert Park on Wireless Road - a tranquil enclave dating back to 1915 - the hotel offers Aman’s notions of peace and serenity, just moments from the bustling heart of the capital.  In calm contrast to the city’s vibrant energy, the property is among the most forward-facing wellness hotels in Bangkok, with a 1,500-square-metre wellness centre alongside seven venues for dining and socialising. Its 36-storeys house 52 spacious suites and 39 branded residences, each designed to envelop guests in the park’s nature, while providing seamless access to Bangkok’s most alluring neighbourhoods; a true embodiment of urban sanctuary.
