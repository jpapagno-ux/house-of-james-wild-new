Amid this remarkable setting, Xigera’s design aesthetic is wholeheartedly dedicated to celebrating African art, design, and creativity. Each piece has been handcrafted and inspired by the incredible natural surroundings – a curated collection of bespoke furniture, sculpture, and artwork by more than 76 African artists and craftsmen, including Andile Dyalvane, Dokter and Misses, Dylan Lewis, Porky Hefer, and Ardmore Ceramics.
