Activities Sunrise and sunset game drives carry a maximum of six guests and careful offroad driving adds a deeper dimension to your experience, with less noise and more time for you to become absorbed in the ways of the wild. Coffee and comfort breaks on the morning drives give guests the opportunity to stretch their legs, refresh and reflect. The evening drives include drinks in idyllic spots - always a fun and delicious way to toast the end of a perfectly beautiful day in Africa. There are also on-foot safaris and star-gazing trips with our masterful guides. If you have any special requests or need more details regarding your preferred activities, chat to your guide when you arrive. 1 / 10 Next Prev Dining Area & Cuisine The culinary delights on offer at Sable Camp are directly informed by local ingredients. Punchy flavours, fresh and nutritious combinations make up the African-inspired dishes, paired with a selection of premium wines. Meals are served in the traditionally-themed dining room, or outdoors in the reed ‘boma’ for magical evenings around the fire. Info Useful Information Health Guests travelling to the Greater Kruger region are encouraged to consult their physician or travel clinic prior to departure to discuss recommended vaccinations and malaria prevention. While the area is considered low risk, simple precautions such as using insect repellent and wearing long sleeves in the evening are advised. Guests should also travel with any required personal medications and ensure appropriate travel insurance is in place. The lodge team is always available to assist should any medical needs arise during your stay. Weather The MalaMala Game Reserve and the surrounding Greater Kruger National Park enjoy a warm, inviting climate year-round, making this region an exceptional safari destination in every season. Summer (October – April) brings warm days, often in the high-20s to mid-30s °C, with dramatic afternoon thunderstorms that refresh the bush and awaken vibrant birdlife, while many animals give birth during this lush, green season. Winter (May – September) offers crisp mornings and mild, sunlit days, with very little rainfall and more open vegetation, allowing for excellent visibility. Wildlife often gathers around the Sand River and nearby water sources, making game viewing particularly rewarding during the dry winter months. Getting there By Air Guests can fly directly to MalaMala’s Private Airstrip with Federal Air, from either Johannesburg or Kruger Mpumalanga International. Private charter flights to this airstrip are also available. This will be followed by a short 15-minute road transfer to the lodge. Alternatively, guests can fly from Johannesburg or Cape Town into Skukuza Airport, situated within the Kruger National Park, followed by a 60-minute road transfer to the lodge. By Road MalaMala Game Reserve is approximately a 5–6 hour drive from Johannesburg. The journey follows well-maintained national and regional roads through the scenic Lowveld to the town of Hazyview, before entering the Sabi Sand Nature Reserve via Shaw’s Gate and continuing on to MalaMala. For further details and personalised arrangements, kindly contact the concierge team. Children Children under the age of 12 may not stay at the camp except in the case of the camp being reserved exclusively. Children aged 12 – 16 are allowed on drives and standard nature walks but not specialist walking safaris. If paying for a private vehicle, children of any age may join game drives and access to sightings is at the discretion of the guide, based on the behaviour of both the child and animals. Greater distances to animals may be adhered to or certain sightings may be avoided, in order to ensure the safety of all. Childminders are available and should be booked on the day of arrival.
