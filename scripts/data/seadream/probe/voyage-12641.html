Overview Ports Staterooms Land Adventures Testimonials

Wrap yourself in the crown jewels of the untouched necklace of islands known as the Grenadines, in the rarely traveled Lesser Antilles. On a journey bookended by Barbados, sail to St. Lucia's Soufriere Bay, defined by its endless stretches of white-sand beaches. Overnight in Bequia, a tiny island of quiet lagoons, reefs, and silvery stretches of beach, often referred to as "the best-kept secret" of the Caribbean. Discover Tobago Cays, a respite for divers and snorkelers, boasting crystal-clear waters, coral reefs, and picture-perfect lagoons. Call on St. George's, Grenada, considered the most beautiful harbor town in the Caribbean, before concluding at Mayreau, just east of the Tobago Cays, where our Champagne & Caviar Splash® unfolds along Salt Whistle Bay, chilled Champagne and caviar giving way to a lively beach barbecue and the aroma of savory lobster.

Saint Lucia 		Morning Evening

Soufriere, a small town on the South West Coast of Saint Lucia is considered by many as the island’s gem. The town and the surrounding region has a population of 8,000. Originally founded by the French, it was the original capital of the island. Soufriere has a great deal to offer visitors including coral reefs, the rainforest, Diamond Falls, the sulfur springs, a “drive-in” volcano and the ever popular and majestic twin pitons. Nov 27, 2026 Mayreau,
St. Vincent and the Grenadines 		Morning Evening

Accessible only by yacht and small sailboats, Mayreau is the smallest public island of the Grenadines, with an area of about .46 square miles and a population of less than 300, centered in an unnamed village on a hilltop in the southwest of the island—its highest point and a nice hike from Saline Beach, where SeaDream anchors. Surrounded by magnificent coral reefs and gorgeous beaches, Mayreau and its surrounding Tobago Cays waters sit in a nationally protected area that has experienced a resurgence of marine life, including turtles that swim and feed while you mingle with and observe them. Take in the flora and fauna and don’t miss some of the best snorkeling in the world. Perhaps the ideal spot to anchor, Saline Bay provides easy access to Saline Bay Beach and Salt Whistle Bay Beach, a rare double-sided beach.

  • Soufriere Bay - Pitons Vistas: Cruise, Swim & Culinary Delights

    Description

    Experience a scenic coastal cruise with breathtaking Piton views, revitalizing swims, and snorkeling at two enchanting St. Lucian bays, and a delicious lunch at a picturesque restaurant perfectly positioned to overlook the majestic Pitons.

    Start your adventure at Soufriere pier, where you'll board your private catamaran set against the breathtaking backdrop of the majestic Pitons. Cruise along the scenic West coast, passing Anse Chastanet/Jade Mountain Resorts and charming fishing villages like Canaries and Anse La Raye. Arrive at Marigot Bay, renowned as one of the Caribbean's most picturesque bays and a hub for yachts worldwide. Enjoy the serene beauty of this remarkable location.

    After Marigot Bay, set sail to Anse Cochon Marine Park for swimming and snorkeling, then head to the stunning Jalousie Bay between the Pitons for a second swim/snorkel stop and some beach time. Return to Soufriere Bay, then embark on a picturesque drive through Soufriere town to continue your unforgettable journey.

    Delight in an authentic St. Lucian culinary experience at an award-winning Farm-to-Table restaurant, perched on a volcano ridgeline 1,000 feet above the azure Caribbean Sea. With breathtaking views of the Pitons, lunch becomes an unforgettable sensory journey. Conclude your day immersed in the unmatched beauty of St. Lucia's landscapes, flavors, and hospitality, a fitting finale to this SeaDream Yachting Land Adventure.

    Notes

    Guests must be in good health and physical condition to participate. This tour is not recommended for guests with heart, respiratory, back, leg, hip, or neck conditions. Guests should bring a towel from the yacht and wear swimwear underneath cool, comfortable clothing, along with flat rubber-soled shoes. There are no changing facilities onboard, and lunch will take place after swimming and boating; casual Caribbean attire is recommended for the restaurant. Please note that there is uphill and downhill walking, as well as several stairs to negotiate at the restaurant. Shade on the catamaran may be limited. Due to the type of buses used, which are Japanese-style buses, legroom may be limited. Minimum age to participate is 10 years old. Children must remain under the supervision of their parent or guardian at all times during the tour. Operation is subject to weather and sea conditions. Depending on availability and group size, a speedboat may be used instead of a catamaran.

  • Soufriere Bay - Soufrière: Anse La Raye E-Bike and Waterfall Hike

    Description

    Explore Saint Lucia’s west coast by e-bike and on foot. Depart the pier in Soufrière for the drive to the fishing village of Anse La Raye, passing through the village of Canaries and past lush countryside, banana plantations and tropical fruit trees. On arrival, guests receive a helmet, an e-bike, and a safety briefing, followed by a practice session. A short, guided ride then leads through the village, past its historic buildings, before continuing along a country road on the outskirts of a secondary forest, where birdsong and the sound of a flowing river accompany the ride.

    At the entrance to the waterfall trail, park the e-bikes and continue on foot with your guide to the waterfall and its river pool. The path crosses uneven ground of dirt and rock through lush forest, where the island’s tropical flora and fauna line the way. Pause at the pool to photograph the waterfall and listen to the cascading water. Retrace the trail to your e-bike and ride back to Anse La Raye, then continue a short distance to the viewpoint for photographs before returning to the village to board your vehicle for the drive back to your yacht.

    Notes

    Guests must be physically fit and free from heart, respiratory, back, leg, hip, or neck conditions. Participants must be between 16 and 70 years of age, between 5'4" (163 cm) and 6'5" (196 cm) in height, and weigh no more than 150 kg (330 lbs). A signed waiver is required, and children must remain under the supervision of a parent or guardian at all times. Each guest is provided with a safety helmet and receives a safety briefing before departure. A practice ride is mandatory to assess balance, braking, turning, and familiarity with the electric assistance. Phone use while riding is prohibited. The group is accompanied by a lead guide and a rear guide, and the riding pace is adjusted to accommodate the least confident rider. The drive between the yacht and the tour site takes approximately 50 minutes each way. Japanese-style buses are used, with limited legroom. Guests should wear cool, comfortable clothing and flat, rubber-soled walking and riding shoes. Surfaces at all stops include uneven concrete, asphalt, and dirt, while the return hike and bike ride from the river pool also involve rocky terrain. The hike is approximately 0.95 km (0.59 miles) each way and includes a river crossing with water depths of approximately 10–30 cm (4 inches–1 foot). The e-bike route is shared with local traffic, with approximately 90% of the ride taking place on a minor road leading to the waterfall and the remainder on a road with slightly more traffic. Guests can adjust the level of motor assistance on the e-bikes.

  • Soufriere Bay - Volcanic Mud Bath & Botanical Gardens

    Description

    Explore the Caribbean's only drive-in volcano at Sulphur Springs Park, relax in warm medicinal mud pools, enjoy a guided tour of the Diamond Botanical Gardens with a quick dip in their warm mineral water pools followed by a tasting of local snacks at a historic Sugar Mill.

    Set off from Soufriere dock on a captivating journey through the historic streets of Soufriere, leading to Sulphur Springs Park, home to the Caribbean's exclusive drive-in volcano. Here you will indulge in the therapeutic warmth of the natural volcanic mud baths. The mud helps draw out toxins from the body, leaving the skin refreshed. Due to its water solubility, the mud in its natural form allows the skin to absorb nutrients from it. Due to the high mineral content, mud baths have been used for thousands of years as medicinal treatments.

    Next continue to the enchanting Diamond Botanical Gardens for a guided tour of the gardens, including an impressive view of the Diamond waterfall. At the Diamond Mineral Baths there is time for a quick dip in the tranquil therapeutic mineral water pools before venturing to the historic Old Sugar Mill. Dating back to 1765, this historic estate factory once processed sugar cane and powered the village of Soufriere with its waterwheel-generated electricity. Enjoy local refreshments amidst its open-air charm before concluding your excursion with a scenic drive back to Soufriere dock to rejoin your SeaDream yacht.

    Notes

    Guests must be in good health and physical condition to participate. This tour is not recommended for guests with heart, respiratory, back, leg, hip, or neck conditions. Guests should wear cool, comfortable clothing and comfortable shoes suitable for walking to the mud baths, touring the Diamond Botanical Gardens, and getting on and off buses. Swimwear should be worn underneath clothing. The mud baths have changing rooms; however, they are rustic and natural, reflecting the outdoor environment. Due to the presence of sulphur in the mud baths, there may be a distinct smell, and swimwear may become discolored. Dark-colored swimwear is recommended. Guests should bring a towel from the yacht, a camera, and insect repellent if allergic or sensitive to tropical conditions.
    Due to the type of buses used, which are Japanese-style buses, legroom may be limited. Children must remain under the supervision of their parent or guardian at all times during the tour. Camouflage clothing is prohibited on the island. The tour sequence may vary.

  • Kingstown - Kingstown Highlights & Local Flavours

    Description

    Discover the history, culture and local flavours of Kingstown on a relaxed journey through the capital aboard an open-air electric sightseeing vehicle. Accompanied by a knowledgeable local guide, travel through the city’s historic streets while learning about St. Vincent’s heritage, traditions and everyday life. Departing from the cruise terminal, begin your sightseeing drive through Kingstown, passing the historic Molasses Tank before arriving at the lively Kingstown Public Market, where you can experience the colourful atmosphere of local daily life and enjoy a tasting of seasonal tropical fruits.

    Continue through the capital, passing the Iron Man Monument before arriving at St. George’s Anglican Cathedral. Enjoy a photo stop while your guide provides a brief introduction to this historic landmark and its place within Kingstown. From here, continue to the St. Vincent Botanical Gardens, where you will enjoy a leisurely guided stroll through the lush tropical surroundings. As you explore, learn more about the gardens and their diverse plant life while taking in the peaceful atmosphere of one of the city’s most inviting green spaces.

    Following your visit to the Botanical Gardens, take some time to relax and enjoy a traditional Vincentian snack accompanied by a refreshing local fruit juice or rum punch. This final stop offers a pleasant opportunity to enjoy another taste of local culture before reboarding your sightseeing vehicle for the short journey back to the cruise terminal and your yacht.

    Notes

    Guests should be prepared to walk approximately 1 miles (1.6 km) in total, including short walking distances, gentle slopes, and outdoor paths and 10 stairs at the Botanical Gardens. Comfortable walking shoes and lightweight clothing are recommended. Guests with limited mobility may participate provided they can board the vehicle and manage the required short walking distances; foldable wheelchairs and electric scooters cannot be accommodated. Transportation is primarily by open-air vehicle without air conditioning or heating. Should this vehicle be unavailable, an 18-seat air-conditioned tour bus may be used instead.   • Port Elizabeth, Bequia - Bequia Coastal Cruise & Snorkel

    Description

    Join your fellow SeaDreamers on a fun snorkeling expedition and scenic cruise along the coastline of Bequia. There is perhaps no better way to enjoy the waters of the Caribbean than a bit of sightseeing and snorkeling, just the right combination to provide a sense of relaxation and adventure at the same time.

    Climb aboard a comfortable catamaran at Port Elizabeth and take in views of Bequia's picturesque waterfront as you set off along the island's coastline. Cruise past the beautiful Princess Margaret Beach and Lower Bay, then keep your camera ready as you pass some rather unusual homes set into the rugged hillside and built from natural materials, an area known as Moonhole. The cruise continues past Petit Nevis and Semple Cay, where you can view Bequia's historic whaling station from the water.

    Your captain will then select a suitable snorkeling location based on the day's weather and sea conditions. Once you're in the water, equipped with vest, mask and snorkel, enjoy time exploring Bequia's underwater environment. Return to the catamaran, where rum punch will be served, and enjoy the scenery as you sip your refreshing drink during the cruise back to Port Elizabeth.

This tour is not recommended for guests with significant mobility or balance limitations. The minimum age to participate is 6 years. Swimming and snorkeling are optional, and guests who swim or snorkel should have basic swimming ability and be comfortable in the water. Prior snorkeling experience makes the tour more enjoyable; however, the crew will assist guests who may need help. Guests who wish to get off the catamaran but cannot snorkel are taken to the beach, and guests are also welcome to stay on board throughout the tour. Snorkeling equipment provided includes a vest, mask and snorkel; fins are not included. The vessel is partly covered, with partially shaded areas onboard. The captain selects the snorkeling location depending on weather and sea conditions. Underwater visibility varies with swell, wind and recent rainfall and can be reduced after heavy rain. Guests are recommended to wear swimwear under comfortable clothing and bring a towel, a hat, sunglasses, non-slip footwear or water shoes, and sunscreen; reef-safe sunscreen is recommended to help protect the marine environment. Rum punch is served onboard after the swimming and snorkeling part of the tour, and guests must be at least 18 years of age to consume alcohol.

  • Port Elizabeth, Bequia - Bequia Sunset Catamaran Cruise

    Description

    Board your catamaran in Port Elizabeth and receive a safety briefing before setting out along Bequia’s coastline. As the vessel leaves the harbour, settle in on deck and take in views of waterfront villages, secluded bays, sandy beaches, and the island’s green hillsides. During the cruise, rum punch and tropical fruit juice are served alongside a selection of light local canapés. The catamaran remains on the water throughout the experience, allowing you to enjoy the coastline from a relaxed vantage point as the afternoon light begins to soften.

At sunset, raise a glass of sparkling wine while taking in the surrounding seascape and the coastline of Bequia. Afterwards, remain aboard for the return journey as twilight settles over the island and the catamaran makes its way back toward Port Elizabeth. On arrival at the harbour, disembark the vessel and conclude the evening after a final view of Bequia from the water.

Comfortable casual evening attire is recommended, together with flat, secure, non-slip footwear, as catamaran decks and boarding areas may become wet.
