Rascal is a traditional handmade phinisi yacht built in 2015
in Sulawesi, Indonesia. Measuring 30m overall, she accommodates  up to 
10 guests across 5 cabins and 6 spacious common areas. With an interior 
that evokes a laid-back luxe vibe reminiscent of the Hamptons, Rascal feels 
like a spacious floating villa.
