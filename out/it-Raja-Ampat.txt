Raja Ampat is quite simply one of the
world’s most beautiful archipelagos;
comprising over 1,500 islands, its
sublime scenery includes fine white-sand
beaches, hidden lagoons and surreal
limestone islets swathed in jungle. 
An unrivalled site for diving and
snorkelling, it is home to a greater
variety of wildlife than any other coastal
region on Earth including 75% of all the
known corals, 17 different marine
mammal species and more than 1,600
species of fish.

Day 1
WHERE WE GO
Sorong to Kali Biru in Waigeo.
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Relax and slip into the Rascal life; trek into the hidden treasures of
Waigeo and explore the crystal-clear waters of Kali Biru; enjoy the
first sunset canapés on the top deck.
WHAT YOU’LL SEE 
The mesmerising blue river of Kali Biru, the lush green jungles of
Waigeo, Papuan hornbills, sea eagles and, with a little luck, the
endemic sail-dragon lizard.
THE HIGHLIGHT 
Take a plunge off the little jetty built by the local village into the
turquoise river of Kali Biru.
TRY YOUR HAND AT
Stargazing on the top deck after dinner.

Day 2
WHERE WE GO
Mioskon, Kri and Mansuar islands in the Dampier Strait.
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Snorkel or dive at Mioskon or Blue Magic (depending on
conditions), Cape Kri and Sawandarek, including a visit to a local
village. Tour the village with the tribal leader and learn how the
community has lived in harmony with the land and ocean for
generations.
WHAT YOU’LL SEE
A dazzling variety of reef fish, wobbegong sharks and the biggest
green turtles you’ve ever seen.
THE HIGHLIGHT
Diving at Cape Kri, where a world record was set for the highest
number of fish species recorded on a single dive. An incredible
374 species were observed.
TRY YOUR HAND AT
Practising your Bahasa Indonesia with the villagers.

Day 3
WHERE WE GO
Yeben archipelago.
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Snorkel or dive vibrant reefs, enjoy water sports, or simply unwind

The Yeben archipelago, thriving reefs, turtles, wobbegong sharks

Having an entire beach to yourself.

===== PAGE 7 =====
WHERE WE GO
Airborek and Penemu, home to extensive reefs and stunning
lagoons.
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Snorkel or dive across some of the most beautiful reefs in the area
including manta point, followed by a gentle trek to Penemu’s
panoramic viewpoint.
WHAT YOU’LL SEE 
Pristine coral shallows, manta rays, bargibant’s pygmy seahorses,
reef sharks, turtles and pelagic fish gliding through the current.
THE HIGHLIGHT 
Getting up close with majestic manta rays.
TRY YOUR HAND AT 
Snorkelling or diving at Melissa’s Garden, a spectacular site
renowned for its vast coral formations and rich marine life.

Day 5
WHERE WE GO
Arrive in Misool to explore Tomolol and Yilliet.
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Begin the morning drifting through a lake filled with thousands of
stingless jellyfish. Continue with a dive or snorkel at Yilliet Kecil
and Romeo for your first glimpse of Misool’s extraordinary
underwater world.
WHAT YOU’LL SEE
One of the world’s few stingless jellyfish lakes, along with schools
of barracuda, fusiliers and batfish, pygmy seahorses, reef sharks
and pristine coral reefs.
THE HIGHLIGHT
Capturing a selfie surrounded by jellyfish against the dramatic
limestone hill backdrop.
TRY YOUR HAND AT
A night dive or snorkel as you search for the endemic Raja
epaulette walking shark.

Day 6
WHERE WE GO
Fiabacet and Tomolol.
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Dive or snorkel at Puri Pinnacle for the chance to see devil rays,
then trek to the top of Pucak Harfat for spectacular views of the
lagoons and islands. End the day with dinner under the stars.
WHAT YOU’LL SEE
If you’re lucky, schools of devil rays hunting bait fish, oceanic
mantas and abundant pelagic life. Enjoy far-reaching views of
endless islands and lagoons from the top of Pucak Harfat.
THE HIGHLIGHT
A magical pop-up dinner on a secluded beach under the stars.
TRY YOUR HAND AT
Visiting the ancient rock art of Tomolol, where you can admire
Aboriginal paintings dating back more than a thousand years.

Day 7
WHERE WE GO
Sagof and Balbulol, home to one of the most breathtaking
lagoons in Raja Ampat.
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Enjoy the final dive or snorkel of your voyage at Two Tree and
explore its dramatic overhang. Spend the afternoon kayaking or
stand-up paddleboarding inside Balbulol’s hidden lagoon before
cooling down with a cold beer.
WHAT YOU’LL SEE
Pygmy seahorses, schooling bumphead parrotfish, reef sharks
and maybe a last surprise from the deep blue. Explore the
dramatic limestone formations and concealed lagoons of
Balbulol.
THE HIGHLIGHT
Sipping your favourite drink from the floating bar while drifting in
the lagoon.
TRY YOUR HAND AT
Fish food golf. Grab a nine iron and a bucket of beers, then hit
balls made of fish food far out into the ocean.

===== PAGE 12 =====
EXTEND YOUR VOYAGE
We recommend a minimum of 7 nights to see Raja Ampat
National Park but if you have the time, this incredible wilderness
area can easily be explored for up to 14 nights. 
+2 NIGHTS
Allows more opportunities to view the flora and fauna of the Bird’s
Head Peninsula – one of the most biodiverse ecoregions on Earth.
+4 NIGHTS
See more of Raja Ampat’s signature dive and snorkel sites in both
the north and south. From manta cleaning stations to bright coral
walls and quiet hidden lagoons, these extra days give you time to
experience a wider range of Raja’s underwater world.
+6 NIGHTS
Continue towards Triton Bay and explore a truly off-grid stretch of
Indonesia, with untouched islands, waterfalls dropping into the
sea, and beautiful whale shark encounters. Finish your journey
disembarking in Kaimana.

===== PAGE 16 =====
All voyages in Raja Ampat start and end in
the port of Sorong on the island of Papua.
There are multiple daily flights from Jakarta
and Bali to Sorong airport, which is just a
short 20-minute drive away. Our crew will
collect you from the airport or your Sorong
accommodation. 
The voyage begins at 7am on the first day
and to ensure a smooth embarkation, we
advise booking the Garuda Indonesia
flight that typically departs Jakarta at 12:30
am or Bali at 01:40 am. The voyage ends at
11 am on the final day and your onward
flight should be scheduled accordingly.  
If you require assistance with flight
schedules, flight bookings (including both
commercial and private), and itinerary
extensions, please don’t hesitate to ask our
friendly team who will be more than happy
to make your travel plans as seamless as
possible.
