This Réserve-Collection luxury safari camp is set in one of the most pristine wilderness areas left on our planet: the banks of Botswana’s Selinda Spillway as it enters the Linyanti River. This area has a sense of remoteness—a true spirit of Africa. Moulded around the trees, Selinda Camp visualises five essential elements: Earth, Wind, Water, Fire and Metal. As a result, Selinda Camp has a perfectly natural sense of place and balance. Read More When Dereck Joubert designed Selinda Camp, with its characteristic indoor/outdoor safari experience, he wanted to pay homage primarily to elephants. The art in the camp starts with life-sized elephant skull sculptures as you enter the main area, reflecting the art of his late brother, Keith Joubert. With only three suites, Selinda Camp is an excellent example of what a perfect luxury safari in Botswana should be like. Each suite is complete with a private open-air verandah and swimming pool—the ensuite bathroom is complete with deep, luxurious brass baths, double vanity basins, a flush loo and indoor showers. Heating and cooling air conditioning facilities are over the sumptuous bed, and the bedroom offers guests a writing desk, coffee and tea-making area, and sufficient wardrobe space. The camp’s main area has a private and relaxing spa area. Guests can also enjoy a range of treatments in the privacy of their suite. The private Selinda Reserve boasts large elephant herds, regular sightings of the Selinda pack of African wild dogs, and the famous Selinda lion pride recently featured in the National Geographic film “Birth of a Pride” by Dereck and Beverly Joubert. Selinda Camp is a proud Relais & Châteaux member. Fellow Réserve-Collection luxury safari camps in Botswana include Zarafa Camp and The Dhow Suite at Zarafa , the Selinda Suite , Sitatunga Private Island and the Sitatunga Private Island Suite , and Duba Plains Camp and the Duba Plains Suite . Our Réserve-Collection luxury safari camps in Zimbabwe include Tembo Plains Camp and The Zanji Suite at Tembo Plains . Our Kenyan Réserve-Collection luxury safari camps are Mara Nyika Camp and The Sambuk Suite at Mara Nyika , Mara Plains Camp and The Jahazi Suite at Mara Plains , Mara Toto Tree Camp , and ol Donyo Lodge . Safari Suite The three expansive, and uniquely designed, guest suites each feature an indoor en-suite bathroom, a large private veranda with outdoor seating and a swimming pool. Read More Beverly Joubert’s fine art photography adorns each suite. Each suite offers you a professional camera, lens set and high-quality binoculars for their complimentary use. At the end of your safari, all the images taken are downloaded for free to take home as our gift. Every suite is inspired by what Livingstone would have had, what he would have travelled with, and what he would have found along with his expeditions. But beyond that, we go back to the Robinson Crusoe feel, including rope textures, suites under thatch, hammocks, lots of space, nautical elements, and salvaged wood. Safari Experiences Our open-vehicle safari drives, guided bush walks, and boating offer opportunities to track and view iconic African wildlife, enjoy bird-watching moments, and participate in other bespoke wildlife-viewing activities. Tag-and-release seasonal fishing is also available. Water-based activities are also water level-dependent at times during the year. Access to the full extent of the Selinda Reserve private concession gives guests the unique ability to experience a wide variety of wildlife experiences and habitat areas. With the permanent waterways that exist, this area is a wildlife magnet throughout the year. It promises to delight guests with the variety of wildlife that is available to be enjoyed. These one-on-one life-changing safari viewing experiences include sightings of leopards, elephants and hippos. They are then hired and deployed in our concessions to monitor and manage the biodiversity. Great Plains’ Female Rangers also offers opportunities to shift gender stereotypes, empower vulnerable women, and establish strong female role models for young girls. The long-term conservation impact of this project is equally powerful; female rangers will help foster the community buy-in necessary to preserve Africa’s most precious biodiversity.
