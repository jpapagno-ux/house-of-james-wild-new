Mesh walls usher the breeze, floods pass beneath walkways, and each camp rests, both nestled amongst the ecosystem and open to its vastness on all sides. Winds change, waters swell and recede, and the sun shifts constantly, revealing a different experience in every moment. Both in the privacy of your camp and the hub at the heart of the property, there’s no separation between inside and out, guest and guide, you and the wild. Here, you learn to see all that you didn’t know to look for. Open by design Wake when you’re ready. Eat when you’re hungry. Go where curiosity leads you. Each camp is completely private, with everything you need to feel at home. Your vehicle is always ready when you are, to set out and explore the landscape. This sense of openness extends to the hub at the heart of the property. Here, shared spaces bring you together, and you can wander into kitchens, pull up a seat in the canteen, and explore the Conservation Lounge and Boutique & Gallery to get a real sense of the people and place behind the experience.
