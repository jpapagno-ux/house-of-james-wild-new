===== PAGE 3 =====
Rebel is a traditional handmade phinisi yacht built in 2020 in Sulawesi, Indonesia. 
Measuring 30m in length, she accommodates up to 10 guests across 5 cabins 
and 6 spacious common areas and includes a fine wine cellar and an outdoor 
cinema under the stars. Inspired by the rich local craft of Indonesia and featuring 
hand-woven fabrics and carvings, the interiors are a blend of modern laid-back 
luxe with a tribal vibe.
