Watch Video Play Video Standing proudly in a forest-like setting of giant riverine trees on the banks of the famed Sand River, Rattray’s Lodge offers genuine seclusion. All eight of the grand award-winning freestanding “villas” overlook the lush floodplains and the river beyond. It forms part of the 13 500ha MalaMala Game Reserve. Activities Guests can choose to pass their time on thrilling game drives, informative nature walks, birdwatching and star-gazing in the company of a professional guide. Twice-daily drives are deliberately unhurried and limited to four or less guests per vehicle. This ensures an intimate journey around an area renowned for its concentrated levels of spectacular sightings and natural splendour. 1 / 11 Next Prev Info Useful Information Health Guests travelling to the Greater Kruger region are encouraged to consult their physician or travel clinic prior to departure to discuss recommended vaccinations and malaria prevention. While the area is considered low risk, simple precautions such as using insect repellent and wearing long sleeves in the evening are advised. Guests should also travel with any required personal medications and ensure appropriate travel insurance is in place. The lodge team is always available to assist should any medical needs arise during your stay. Weather The MalaMala Game Reserve and the surrounding Greater Kruger National Park enjoy a warm, inviting climate year-round, making this region an exceptional safari destination in every season. Summer (October – April) brings warm days, often in the high-20s to mid-30s °C, with dramatic afternoon thunderstorms that refresh the bush and awaken vibrant birdlife, while many animals give birth during this lush, green season. Winter (May – September) offers crisp mornings and mild, sunlit days, with very little rainfall and more open vegetation, allowing for excellent visibility. Wildlife often gathers around the Sand River and nearby water sources, making game viewing particularly rewarding during the dry winter months. Getting there By Air Guests can fly directly to MalaMala’s Private Airstrip with Federal Air, from either Johannesburg or Kruger Mpumalanga International. Private charter flights to this airstrip are also available. This will be followed by a short 20-minute road transfer to the lodge. Alternatively, guests can fly from Johannesburg or Cape Town into Skukuza Airport, situated within the Kruger National Park, followed by a 45-minute road transfer to the lodge. By Road MalaMala Game Reserve is approximately a 5–6 hour drive from Johannesburg. The journey follows well-maintained national and regional roads through the scenic Lowveld to the town of Hazyview, before entering the Sabi Sand Nature Reserve via Shaw’s Gate and continuing on to MalaMala. For further details and personalised arrangements, kindly contact the concierge team. Children Rattray’s Lodge does not allow children under the age of 16 as it is designed specifically for a tranquil safari experience for adults. However, children over the age of 12 will be allowed if the entire ramp is exclusively reserved.
