 A FIRST TIME OPPORTUNITY
 No other yacht management company started as a hospitality brand.
 As an established luxury brand, Rascal Voyages offers a fresh approach to yacht management, transforming an expense into a revenue generating opportunity.
 TURN KEY YACHT AND BRAND MANAGEMENT SERVICES
 In addition to managing your charter operation, we are fully licensed and equipped to provide turnkey vessel management services.
 We can convert your operation into a Rascal Voyages branded yacht.
 Our two specialist teams work alongside each other from our Bali headquarters. 
 TWO SPECIALIST TEAMS 
 Our services are split into two arms:
 Yacht Management covers every aspect of operational administration and compliance of your vessel.
 Brand Management ensures that every aspect of your charter operation is run in line with Rascal Voyages luxury brand standards.
