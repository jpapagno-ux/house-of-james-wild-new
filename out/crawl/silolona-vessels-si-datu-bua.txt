 Si Datu Bua, meaning "Beloved Princess," stands as the latest masterpiece by Silolona Sojourns, crafted by the revered Konjo boat builders of Sulawesi. This magnificent vessel is designed by Seery Yacht Design, seamlessly blending the timeless charm of traditional island sailing with modern amenities and safety, setting the standard for excellence.
 From the tranquil Sulawesi Sea to the majestic Toradja mountains, Si Datu Bua offers a unique journey, complemented by our meticulously restored 164-year-old clan house on Gunung Sesean's slopes. Step aboard to discover tastefully appointed interiors, spacious decks, delightful Asian fusion cuisine, and a full array of aquatic activities. Si Datu Bua is your gateway to the ultimate luxury yacht charter in Indonesia, where each moment is a cherished memory of exploration and opulence.
