Overview Ports Staterooms Land Adventures Testimonials

The Bahamas burst with their abundant stunning beaches, untouched nature, and distinct biodiversity—nestled in an archipelago of sheltered cays and islets, best discovered from the perspective of your intimate SeaDream yacht. Sail roundtrip from Palm Beach, and begin by exploring untouched Andros Island, where mangrove estuaries and underwater caves have created haven for scuba divers and snorkelers. Discover Great Exuma Island, its waters so clear and aquamarine-blue they are visible from space, and its powdery white-sand beaches, deserted cays, and exclusive golf resorts fringing the coastline. Relax under the shade of pine trees at Gordon’s Beach, the southernmost white-sand stretch on Long Island. Explore the isle from the perspective two of its most impressive natural wonders, the Hamilton Caves, dripping with stalactites, and Dean’s Blue Hole, the world’s second-deepest saltwater blue hole, surrounded by limestone bluffs. Uncover San Salvador, one of the Bahamas’ smallest inhabited islands, boasting sparkling inland lakes, reef-filled sea waters, miles of secluded white-sand beaches, and dive sites teeming with sea life. Swim with native pigs in the unsung paradise of Staniel Cay and explore Grand Bahama’s Taino Beach, a secluded escape of soft-white sand.

Mar 21, 2027 Andros Island,
Bahamas 		Afternoon Evening

The untouched island of Andros is nestled in the Bahamas, defined by its archipelago of tiny cays and inlets connected by mangrove estuaries and underwater caves, making it accessible only to boats and small sailing vessels. A favorite of scuba divers and snorkelers, Barrier Reef and Tongue of the Sea is the third largest barrier reef in the world, stretching more than 140 miles long. Just off the shores of Andros, this reef teems with exotic fish species. Home to five national parks, more than 180 blue holes, biodiverse ecosystems, and a network of trails that cross the Central Andros National Park, Andros Island is perfect for nature lovers and hikers alike.

The island is also home to St. Mary the Virgin Anglican Church, a now-abandoned stone church constructed by the Spanish in the 1600s and reputed to be the oldest in the Bahamas.

A stone cross on the shores of Long Bay mark Columbus’s landfall on the island; offshore, an underwater monument designates where La Pinta dropped anchor. Pace yourself to the rhythm of the island with a leisurely swim or bike ride around the bay.

  • Emerald Bay, Great Exuma Island - Emerald Bay ATV Adventure (Double ATV)

    Description

    Experience Great Exuma in a unique and thrilling way on a guided two-seat ATV tour across the island. Big things come in small packages, and that saying was never truer than in Great Exuma, just 37 stunning miles long and ready to be explored.

    The convoy sets off with a stop at Pjs Farm, a taste of authentic island life before the adventure really begins. From there, discover the best of Great Exuma's history: St Margaret's Anglican Church, one of the oldest churches in the country, built in 1848, and the Pompey Steventon Museum, the island's first prison, named in honor of Pompey, a courageous slave who lived on the Rolle Plantation at Steventon.

    Next, the trail opens up to Exuma's wild side, a scenic cove known for sea turtle sightings, followed by Isaac Bay, a hidden beach tucked away from the crowds. Venture on to a natural cave that once sheltered locals from storms and, legend has it, pirates hiding their treasure.

    The beaches in Exuma are incredibly beautiful, with crystal clear waters to match. Stop at Exuma Point Beach to take it all in, then ride the off-trail road to Coco Plum Beach for a fresh coconut snack and the chance to experience true paradise, fine white sand, fresh sea air, and the perfect finish before heading back to the ATV base and the yacht.

    Notes

    The price of this YLA is per vehicle, not per person. Each ticket covers one two-seat ATV and is valid for up to two guests. Drivers must be at least 25 years old and hold a valid driver’s license to operate the ATV.

    This tour is not wheelchair accessible. Guests should expect a moderate level of physical activity, including walking on sand, cobblestones, and steps. Guests who use a wheelchair should be aware that participation is not possible due to the terrain and mobility requirements described above. ATV touring in the Bahamas involves unpaved, rural roads that may be dusty or uneven. Turtle sightings at the Cove are wildlife-dependent and cannot be guaranteed, as sea turtles are wild animals and sightings vary by day, tide, and season.The minimum age to participate is 5 years, and the maximum weight allowance is 204 kg (450 lbs). A waiver is required to participate.
    Closed-toe shoes, water shoes, and sun-protective clothing are recommended. Sunscreen, hats, and sunglasses are strongly advised due to extended time outdoors and multiple stops at sandy, unshaded beaches.

    This Yachting Land Adventure must be confirmed no later than 48 hours (2 days) prior to arrival in port; bookings may still be accepted after this deadline, up to the time of arrival, if space is available. Please note that cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival in port are non‑refundable. A minimum number of participants is required to operate all Yachting Land Adventures, and some may have limited capacity. Please book on the Seadream.com website, or onboard, early to avoid disappointment. All adventures in the SeaDream Yachting Land Adventures program are subject to change. Final departure times will be published on board. YLAs operate rain or shine. Guests are encouraged to reserve online in advance, as tour availability may be limited once onboard.

  • Gordon's Beach, Long Island - Hamilton Caves and Dean’s Blue Hole

    Description

    Explore Long Island and experience two of its most impressive natural wonders – one dripping with stalactites, the other plunging 663 feet down into the world's second-deepest saltwater blue hole!

    Step aboard your air-conditioned bus at Gordon's Beach for an island exploration seeing its highlights: Gun Hill, the highest point in Clarence Town and the historic St. Peter and Paul church with stunning views of the harbor.

    Next, travel along the scenic Queen's Highway, to the world-famous Dean's Blue Hole. Surrounded by limestone bluffs and a splendid white-sand beach, this setting is idyllic for relaxing along the unspoiled Caribbean shore. But the real attraction here is to wade, or swim out, through the shallows to where the sandy seafloor suddenly disappears – suddenly dropping 663 feet into the world's second-deepest saltwater blue hole. Or dare to be brave and adventurous by climbing up the rocky pathway and plunge into the blue depths below.

    Afterwards, you will return to your bus for a short drive to Hamilton's Cave. Your expert guide leads you to survey the labyrinthine of darkness in the Bahamas' largest cave system. Multiple chambers open to reveal impressive stalactites and stalagmites formed over centuries as well as many bats who (fortunately) mostly slumber during the daytime in the crevices of the caves.

    All too soon it is time to rejoin your bus for the 30-minute drive to the yacht waiting at Gordon's Beach.

    Notes

    This Yachting Land Adventure includes walking for approximately 1 hour over uneven ground with limited light at the cave and driving over somewhat rough road to reach Dean's Blue Hole beach. Please wear flat, comfortable shoes suitable for the cave excursion, and dress in swimwear with cover-up for the beach visit. Be sure to bring along a hat, sunscreen, and your towel from the yacht. There is a restroom break on the tour. We recommend you bring US dollars to purchase any food or drink from the small local vendors. Please note the itinerary may be operated in reverse order.

    This Yachting Land Adventure must be confirmed no later than 48 hours (2 days) prior to arrival in port; bookings may still be accepted after this deadline, up to the time of arrival, if space is available.   • San Salvador - Parasol Rouge Experience with Lobster Lunch

    Description

    Dive into the breathtaking natural beauty and vibrant marine life of San Salvador's stunning tidal lagoon. Your adventure begins with a short drive to Pigeon Creek & Snow Bay National Park on the southeast coast of San Salvador. Here, you will board a pontoon boat for a scenic journey through a coastal paradise.

    Relax and let the serene surroundings of Pigeon Creek wash over you. The lagoon's sparkling turquoise waters are fringed by mangrove forests and pristine white sands. As you glide through this tranquil haven, watch for starfish, turtles, rays, sharks, and colorful tropical fish. Birdwatchers may spot species like herons, egrets, cormorants, Bahama Woodstar hummingbirds, and Bananaquit songbirds.

    Your journey continues to the magnificent sandbank at Low Cay Sound, a dream-worthy oasis of powdery white sand surrounded by crystal-clear waters. This enchanting sandbar is perfect for swimming and snorkeling, offering a vibrant underwater world to explore or a serene escape to simply unwind and soak in the beauty.

    Experience true Bahamian hospitality with a sumptuous lunch by the water's edge. Enjoy grilled lobster brushed with garlic lemon butter, Angus beef kebobs, pasta, and a garden salad. Complement your meal with wine, beer, rum punch, and refreshing beverages while enjoying island music that creates a festive atmosphere.

    After a day of sun and fun, cruise over the mesmerizing shades of blue back to San Salvador to rejoin your SeaDream yacht.

  • San Salvador - Snorkeling San Salvador's Coral Gardens

    Description

    Set out from the pier aboard a catamaran bound for two of San Salvador's finest reef sites, where the water is celebrated for a clarity found in few places in the Caribbean. A certified snorkel instructor accompanies you into the shallows, where coral gardens open below in amber, violet and gold. Soft corals and sea fans sway with the current, orange and red starfish rest on floors of white sand, and sponges and reef fish crowd the formations rising from the seabed below you.

    At the second site, tidal currents have carved the rock into archways and sheltered channels alive with juvenile fish. Parrotfish graze along the coral heads while blue tangs, angelfish, trumpetfish and sergeant majors drift past in open water. No previous experience or diving certification is required, and all equipment is provided. Afterward, return to the catamaran for the crossing back to the marina, stepping ashore at the pier two hours after setting out, with San Salvador's underwater world still vivid in mind.

    Notes

    This is an active excursion recommended only for guests in good physical health. The minimum age is 8 years, and all guests must sign a waiver in order to participate. Guests must negotiate 3 to 5 steps to embark and disembark the vessel. Snorkeling takes place directly from the boat.

  • Staniel Cay, Exumas - Bahamas Famous Swimming Pigs

    Description

    Meet the famous Bahamas Swimming Pigs at Pig Beach on Pig Island during this exciting Yachting Land Adventure. Visit Pigs Beach where you will see that pigs cannot fly but they sure can swim!

    In the middle of paradise, with billionaires and celebrities for neighbors, is an island populated only by swimming pigs. For decades, this archipelago of 365 islands would remain largely unknown to the world. It would not be a ruthless pirate, pioneering loyalists, a notorious drug kingpin, or the infamous Fyre Festival that would unveil Exuma to the world, but rather the most unlikely of creatures. Appearing in magazines, videos, newspapers, commercials, TV shows, and countless selfies, are the Swimming Pigs of Staniel Cay Exuma, in the Bahamas. They have become a bucket-list sensation and have been named one of the marvels of the universe. After your pig encounter you will be transferred back to the yacht with lots of photos, selfies, and memories to share with your fellow SeaDreamers and family onboard.

    Notes

    Towels, ice cold water and soft drinks, snacks for humans and the pigs are provided. Professionally maintained snorkeling gear is also available. This trip is not suitable for expectant mothers, infants, or people with back, knee or neck problems, heart problems or conditions affected by high-speed travel. All participants should be physically fit and know how to swim. Participants must also be able to understand basic English and be able to respond to emergency commands in the unlikely event they are required during an excursion. Every participant must sign a liability waiver before joining the tour.

    Late afternoon departures may find some pigs lounging on the beach because of their full bellies from earlier feedings.   • Taino Beach, Grand Bahama - Glass Bottom Snorkel Adventure

    Description

    Discover the fascinating underwater world of Grand Bahama Island on this two-in-one marine adventure, combining snorkeling at a vibrant coral reef with a scenic glass-bottom boat exploration. After a short tender ride to Flamingo Bay Marina, stroll along the wooden pier to board the Ocean View, a custom-built, double-deck glass-bottom boat. Following a warm welcome and safety briefing from the friendly crew, set off across the crystal-clear Caribbean waters toward Rainbow Reef, home to colorful tropical fish and beautiful coral formations.

    Upon arrival, enjoy approximately 40 minutes of snorkeling in the clear, inviting waters, observing the vibrant marine life beneath the surface. The crew will provide snorkeling equipment and instructions, with life jackets and masks required for all participants and fins available as an option. For those who prefer to stay dry, snorkeling is entirely optional. Relax onboard, soak up the sunshine, or admire the panoramic ocean views from the upper deck while fellow guests explore the reef.

    Back onboard, continue your adventure with a 40-minute narrated glass-bottom boat tour. Look through the vessel's underwater viewing windows as you cruise above the Papa Doc shipwreck and Shark Junction, discovering fascinating marine life and hidden underwater sights without getting wet. During the scenic 25-minute return journey to the marina, unwind with complimentary water, fruit punch, or lemonade while admiring Grand Bahama's picturesque coastline before taking the tender back to your yacht.

    Notes

    This excursion is suitable for guests with a moderate level of physical fitness. Guests must walk approximately 50 feet (15 meters) along a wooden pier to board the vessel, and six steps lead to the upper deck. The boat is not wheelchair accessible and is not equipped with elevators, lifts, or ramps. The minimum age for snorkeling is 8 years. Participants must be confident swimmers and able to enter and exit the water using the boat's ladder. For safety reasons, guests with mobility limitations or heart or chronic respiratory conditions are not permitted to participate. All guests are required to sign a waiver upon boarding. Flash photography is not permitted at the reef. Sunscreen is recommended, and guests are welcome to bring their own snorkeling equipment if preferred.
