Historic pictures integrate with a sensual spectrum of lifestyle elements from the past that tell the stories of a bygone era. Watch Video Play Video This immaculate camp was named after W. Harry Kirkman, one of the founders of the Sabi Sand Game Reserve who transitioned from being a hunter to conservationist and became a ranger in the Kruger National Park from 1933. It reflects the down-to-earth spirit of pioneer days, yet offers contemporary amenities for total comfort and excellent sightings. Tea-time on the rolling lawn is a nostalgic experience and, if interesting old books are your thing, the library is the ideal retreat. The vintage sitting-room is a good place to meet up for pre-dinner drinks and the cool, wide verandas are perfect for leisurely lunches or breakfasts after your game drive. Wellness Between game drives, slow down and enjoy the simple pleasures of the bush. Pick up a racket for a friendly game of tennis, or gather on the lawn for a fun round of boule, croquet and other favourites. Or simply settle into the camp’s peaceful rhythm. For those who want to unwind even further, relaxing massages are available in the Treatment Room. Cuisine Dining at Kirkman’s is a nostalgic affair. Wholesome global favourites are complemented by authentic Pan-African dishes, all prepared using fresh, locally sourced produce. A thoughtfully curated range of premium wines elevates every meal. Enjoy dining in the convivial dining room, in the boma around the campfire, or on the surrounding veranda - each setting framed by a romantic, candle-lit ambience. Activities There are sunrise and sunset game drives, with exclusive traversing across 10 500ha of pristine land to explore. Leopards are particularly prolific in this area and even the most seasoned traveller will agree there is nothing to match the thrill of spotting that flick-of-a-tail or twitching ear prior to seeing one of these magnificent cats slinking into full view. Always a breath-taking event! Coffee and sundowner interludes in idyllic locations punctuate every drive. If you are keen to mix things up and explore on foot as well, we offer interpretive bush and nature walks accompanied by your guide. Exclusive safaris for groups can be easily organised and there’s croquet and tennis on the sprawling lawn for energetic guests. 1 / 10 Next Prev Info Useful Information Health Guests travelling to the Greater Kruger region are encouraged to consult their physician or travel clinic prior to departure to discuss recommended vaccinations and malaria prevention. While the area is considered low risk, simple precautions such as using insect repellent and wearing long sleeves in the evening are advised. Guests should also travel with any required personal medications and ensure appropriate travel insurance is in place. The lodge team is always available to assist should any medical needs arise during your stay. Weather The Sabi Sand enjoys a warm, inviting climate year-round, making it a perfect safari destination in every season. Summer (October - April) brings warm days, often in the high 20s to mid-30s °C, with dramatic afternoon thunderstorms that refresh the bush and awaken vibrant birdlife and newborn wildlife. Winter (May - September) offers crisp mornings and mild, sunlit days, with minimal rainfall and open vistas that make game viewing on the river and nearby waterholes especially rewarding. Getting there By Air Guests can fly from Johannesburg or Cape Town into Skukuza Airport, situated within the Kruger National Park, followed by a short road 15 – 20 minute transfer to the lodge. Alternatively, Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport offers convenient access to the reserve. Private charter flights to nearby airstrips are also available. By Road The lodge is accessible via a scenic drive from Johannesburg along established routes into the reserve, offering a gradual transition from city to wilderness. For further details and personalised arrangements, kindly contact the concierge team. Children Kirkman's Kamp welcomes children of all ages, with specific policies for pricing, accommodation, and activities. Youngsters under 5 stay for free, while those aged 6–16 receive a 50% discount when sharing with full-paying adults. While little ones under 5 are not permitted on standard game drives, babysitters can be arranged at an extra cost. Pre-teens are not allowed on bush walks, but childminders can be arranged on the day of arrival, allowing parents to enjoy uninterrupted moments in the bush.
