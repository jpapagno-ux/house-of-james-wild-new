Safari Suite The 2-bedroomed, private Jahazi Suite at Mara Plains is the epitome of a private Kenya safari and is perfect for those seeking the ultimate experience on their Maasai Mara adventure. Read More Guests enter the Jahazi Suite at Mara Plains through beautifully carved, ancient Zanzibari doors, which lead into a central private lounge and dining area. Two large, expansive guest bedrooms flank this central area. Each bedroom has a walk-through wardrobe, double basins, a private toilet, an indoor shower and a brass bath. Discreet over-bed air conditioning and ceiling fans above the expansive beds provide additional comfort, while writing desks invite guests to capture their thoughts and inspirations during their stay with us. A private wrap-around verandah with seating and a private swimming pool leads out from the central area. There is little in this world more unforgettable than having a morning coffee or afternoon swim in a private Suite under the trees, watching Africa’s iconic wildlife stroll by. The Jahazi Suite at Mara Plains offers a high-quality pair of binoculars and a professional camera with a selection of lenses for your complimentary use. Following your safari stay with us, your wildlife images and video footage will be shared with you via a private online gallery as our gift for you to enjoy at home. The private Olare Motorgi Conservancy lies on the northern boundary of Kenya’s Maasai Mara National Reserve. Guests of Mara Plains Camp and the private Jahazi Suite at Mara Plains have access to wildlife viewing in this private conservancy, as well as the Maasai Mara National Reserve. Mara Plains is a sanctuary for resident wildlife species, including many of the Maasai Mara’s 300 bird species. After dark, larger mammals frequent the camp, and Maasai warriors keep a watchful eye out while guests sleep. The Jahazi Suite at Mara Plains is a showcase for one of the world’s most magnificent wildlife havens. We believe in keeping the safari experience intimate and personal, so there is no set camp schedule or mealtimes. Your preferences and wildlife opportunities of the moment tailor the day’s activities and dining arrangements. Begin Your Journey Lasting Impact Building Bridges: Connect communities with access to healthcare, education and markets The rains in the Masai Mara cause fast-flowing rivers, often cutting off communities from schools, clinics, markets and more. In the rainy season, children would miss school because the water levels were too dangerous to cross, setting them back in their education. In partnership with Bridging the Gap Africa, Great Plains is building safe and durable bridges, creating a reliable access point across the rivers. The impact of our Building Bridges initiative has been more significant than we imagined, with communities now having year-round access to schools, healthcare, and markets.
