The rooms all have underfloor heating. The generous marble-clad en-suite bathrooms have rain showers and heated towel rails as standard. Careful attention to detail is prevalent throughout the property. Explore Book Now THE DINING ROOM At Leeu Estates Promising exclusivity, The Dining Room is open only to resident guests at Leeu Estates, where they can enjoy breakfast, lunch and dinner in a tranquil and private setting. The seasonal menu will make use of the abundance of fresh produce grown in the gardens at Leeu Estates. Guests visiting Leeu Spa and Mullineux & Leeu Family Wine Studio at Leeu Estates are now able to join us for lunch or dinner at The Dining Room. Explore LA PETITE COLOMBE At Leeu Estates La Colombe, the award-winning and world-renowned restaurant based in Constantia, one of the oldest winemaking regions of South Africa, established a sister restaurant at Leeu Estates to bring its acclaimed experience to the Franschhoek valley. Explore LEEU SPA At Leeu Estates Set within the gardens, vineyards and art of Leeu Estates, Leeu Spa is a quiet expression of place. Here, intentional architecture, natural light and views across the Franschhoek Valley create space to pause and return to yourself. Every detail - from tranquil treatment rooms and water spaces to rituals shaped by skilled hands - has been considered with care. Rooted in presence, craftsmanship and the restorative intelligence of touch, Leeu Spa invites you not to become someone new, but to remember what has always been within. Explore THE WINE STUDIO At Leeu Estates In partnership with Chris and Andrea Mullineux, the talented husband and wife team behind the world-renowned wines produced on Roundstone Farm in the Swartland appellation as well as at Leeu Estates in Franschhoek, Mullineux & Leeu Family Wines offer immersive tastings at The Wine Studio at Leeu Estates. There are three tutored tasting options to choose from: Signature, Single Terroir and Leeu Passant. All curated tastings are presented by dedicated hosts who will guide guests in the finer nuances of these premium handcrafted wines and share their knowledge of new vintage releases or special cuvées. One complimentary tasting per Leeu Estates guest per stay is offered, and to avoid disappointment, booking is advised. Explore Book Now EVERARD READ GALLERY At Leeu Estates Everard Read’s newest gallery at Leeu Estates augments the existing gallery in the high street of Franschhoek. The focus is on showing monumental sculpture in the breathtaking fynbos and vineyards of Leeu Estates, as well as exhibiting important works by its artists in the purpose-built gallery on site. The Fynbosch quarter, where the new gallery is located on the farm, was designed by DHK architects, who also designed the Norval Foundation in Steenberg. The gallery benefits from their experience garnered there, with understated but immaculate finishes that show the art to its best advantage, while offering glimpses of the spectacular natural environment that surrounds Leeu Estates. The gallery complex includes a studio and cottage for an artist residency programme which will provide a tranquil retreat for artists to focus on developing new ideas and generate interesting content for the gallery. Explore #LEEULOCALLYGROWN At Leeu Estates The Leeu edible garden initiative was begun under the guidance of Christine Stevens. A series of vegetable, herb and fruit gardens were planted at Leeu Estates to supply the restaurants, hotel and spa. All the produce on the property is grown following natural farming principles.   Explore INDABA – THE MEETING PLACE At Leeu Estates Indaba – The Meeting Place at Leeu Estates is a state-of-the-art multipurpose event and meeting venue available for all in-house Leeu Collection guests. The light-filled space, with its open roof trusses, tall windows, timber floors and natural finishes, perfectly complements the collection of art and sculptures. Indaba, which is 140m2/1 510ft2 in size and has its own lift, boasts audio and video conferencing equipment, a 203-centimetre (80-inch) television screen and high-speed wifi. The well-appointed venue can accommodate guests both inside and on the terrace overlooking the beautiful valley and mountain landscape for a variety of private parties, exhibitions and product launches. Press briefings, seminars, business meetings, and management think tank sessions can be held in boardroom, schoolroom, or U-shaped seating. Cinema seating can accommodate up to 60 guests. The Dining Room culinary team caters for Indaba.
