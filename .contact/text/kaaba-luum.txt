Where community deepens under the sky. Elemental villas Where you'll be staying Five villas, each named for an element — from a studio casita to three-bedroom houses with rooftop terraces. Most have a flexible downstairs, so the sleeping arrangement bends to your group. Together, the estate holds up to 36. Villa Tierra Earth · Grounding The element of arriving. Stone, timber, and the deepest shade on the property — the villa that sits closest to the jungle floor. Where you put the people who need to land slowly. Sleeps 4–8 · Two queen suites + flexible downstairs Villa Agua Water · Release The element of softening. Quiet light, soft textures, and a room that works on your nervous system before you notice. Where we put the guest who insists she's fine. Sleeps 4–8 · Two queen suites + flexible downstairs Villa Aire Air · Clarity The element of perspective. The most light and the most breeze on the property, with the canopy moving at eye level. The villa people take their coffee outside in. Sleeps 4–8 · Two queen suites + flexible downstairs Villa Fuego Fire · Transformation The element of gathering. Where the warmth collects after dark — the villa the group somehow ends up in, long after everyone said they were going to bed. Sleeps 4–8 · Two queen suites + flexible downstairs Casita Ether · Home base The element of holding space. Two fully private bedrooms — one king, one with two singles, each with its own full bath — around a shared kitchen, living and dining space with its own deck and plunge pool. Where retreat hosts stay, and the natural overflow for families. Sleeps 4 · Two private bedrooms + shared living Inside a villa · the downstairs One villa, three ways. Upstairs never changes: two private queen suites, each with its own full bath, a small terrace, and its own entrance from outside — so those rooms stay genuinely private. It's the downstairs that moves. One shared room, three configurations, set up for your group before you arrive. Option One Full lounge The shared room stays open — couches, the kitchen and breakfast bar, and glass on both ends onto the plunge pool. For groups who want somewhere to land together that isn't someone's bedroom. Sleeping added None · living space Option Two Half lounge, half king Half the room keeps its living space, the other half becomes a king bed. The configuration most groups land on — you gain a bed without losing the room everyone gathers in. Sleeping added One king · +2 guests Option Three Two king beds The room converts fully into sleeping space with two king beds. For groups travelling at capacity who'd rather have the beds than the lounge. Sleeping added Two kings · +4 guests Every configuration keeps the same downstairs: a full bath with shower and closet space, a full kitchen, laundry, and glass walls front and back that open straight onto your own plunge pool.
