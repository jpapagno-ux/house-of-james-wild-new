The Hotel Villa Honegg is a unique 5-star superior boutique hotel with 25 exquisitely furnished rooms. With its exclusive offer and exposed location, it is unique in Switzerland. The Art Nouveau villa on the Bürgenstock was built in 1905 and reopened in May 2011 after a complete renovation, without losing any of its historic charm. High above Lake Lucerne, you will find relaxation and privacy in a unique setting.   The rooms at the Villa Honegg Featuring a pleasant blend of natural materials and elegance, the decor and furnishings create an inviting ambience fostering relaxation and a sense of well-being.
